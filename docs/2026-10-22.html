二、分段双语内容导读

00:04–01:15｜把紧迫问题转成可行动的问题

原创英文概述： Stewart introduces severe material deprivation as an urgent moral problem, then presents the possibility of change. He challenges the assumption that progress will arrive automatically through growth, new inventions or expert organisations.

中文解释： 开场先让听众感到问题严重，再给出希望，避免讨论停在无力感上。00:28 转向可能性，00:42 追问为何未能解决。对技术与专家的质疑是引入主张的方式，不能理解成技术或专业知识毫无价值。

01:15–02:51｜专业经历与统计尺度

原创英文概述： His development career establishes a basis for criticising aid practices. He then distinguishes a falling poverty share from a growing number of affected people in parts of Africa, using this contrast to question whether existing approaches are sufficient.

中文解释： 讲者把自己放进被批评的体系，增强自我反思的可信度。02:03 开始的比例与人数对比尤其适合雅思：总人口变化时，两种指标可向不同方向移动。不过，从人数增长到项目失效的归因还需要其他证据；中国增长与非洲数字是演讲中的历史叙述。

02:51–04:20｜一个学校项目中的投入与结果

原创英文概述： A school sanitation visit illustrates a gap between elaborate project preparation and the modest facilities eventually provided. The story makes the opportunity cost of administration visible through a concrete comparison between spending and delivery.

中文解释： 原本的机制是改善学校卫生设施，帮助女生持续上学。厚报告、车辆和现场设施形成鲜明反差，把抽象效率问题变成可想象的场景。讲者报告项目费用约四万美元、实际设施价值最多约两千美元；这是一则个人见闻，不是对所有卫生项目的随机评估。

04:20–05:04｜风险控制也有机会成本

原创英文概述： The response to his question reveals a fear that local managers might misuse funds. Stewart redirects attention towards the resources absorbed by the aid system itself, arguing that the same budget could have reached more schools.

中文解释： 04:29 给出防止资金被挪用的理由，04:41 后讲者用强烈的道德措辞反击。其“机构拿走了钱”的表述主要谴责浪费，不能据此认定具体人员构成法律意义的盗窃。04:45 有掌声标签；标签只说明字幕事件，不能替代声音分析。

05:04–05:56｜检查一个熟悉观念的前提

原创英文概述： He links programme design to institutional incentives and inherited assumptions. A familiar lesson about teaching skills becomes the target of criticism because it can assume that recipients lack knowledge rather than resources.

中文解释： 从项目故事上升到心智模型：援助者是否把“缺资源”误判成“不会做”？05:40 开始说明这种观念可能产生居高临下的方案。讲者挑战的是把培训当作默认答案的做法，并未证明所有技能培训无效。

05:56–07:05｜现金项目使讲者改变判断

原创英文概述： A later visit near the Rwanda–Burundi border introduces a contrasting approach: households receive a single cash grant without prescribed purchases. Improvements he observes in housing, schooling and household security make him reconsider his initial scepticism.

中文解释： 一次性约九百美元的款项既不是每月工资，也不是贷款。06:39 起列举村庄变化，将讲者由怀疑到支持的转折具体化。这是观察和感受，缺少本段自身的对照组；后文研究部分才尝试提供更强的支持，不能把前后变化直接全部归因于现金。

07:05–08:02｜用研究回应偶然性与滥用担忧

原创英文概述： Stewart introduces random allocation and comparison groups to move beyond an impressive visit. He reports improvements across several outcomes, benefits extending to nearby communities, and findings that challenge the expectation of widespread unproductive spending.

中文解释： 研究转述的作用是回应“是否只是幸运个案”。讲者将现金与健康、教育、收入等结果联系，并报告周边经济收益。演讲没有在这一段展开各研究的样本、时期和估计方法；乘数不能当成每次捐款的固定回报，也不能推出任何场景都没有价格上涨或滥用。

08:02–08:37｜机制一：可能缺的是资本

原创英文概述： A shopkeeper example separates business knowledge from the ability to buy stock. When the binding constraint is money for inputs, another course may do less than access to funds that the person can use immediately.

中文解释： 以经营杂货店为例，讲者把“知道怎么经营”和“买得起存货”拆开。这是一个机制解释，适合写作中说明政策为何可能有效；判断真正约束仍需当地资料，不能假设每个人都已经具备全部知识。

08:37–09:09｜机制二：需求各不相同

原创英文概述： Different residents prioritise education, healthcare, retail or transport businesses. The variety supports flexible assistance because a uniform package cannot easily match every household's circumstances.

中文解释： 多个人物需求并列，使“个性化”由口号变成具体差别。09:07 为掌声和笑声标签。写作文不必背姓名，保留“同一社区也存在多种需求”的逻辑即可；选择自由与所有选择都有良好结果是两件事。

09:09–09:52｜机制三与四：成本和自主权

原创英文概述： Local repairs illustrate how households may achieve useful results with fewer resources. He then adds a distinct argument: people who control a decision may take greater responsibility for maintaining what they have chosen.

中文解释： 09:09 讨论本地材料、邻里协作如何降低成本；09:31 转向信任、维护意愿与自豪感。前者是效率理由，后者兼有行为机制和价值判断。两个理由相互支持，却不能混成“便宜所以必然有尊严”。

09:52–11:00｜技术扩大送达与提前应对的可能

原创英文概述： Mobile payments are presented as a way to deliver assistance more directly. Examples involving targeting and flood preparation extend the argument from transferring money to reaching people quickly and helping them act before a crisis.

中文解释： 10:23 开始提多哥发款和提前应对洪水的例子；这些是演讲所述案例，未在本篇独立核验。技术承担“如何送达、何时送达”的角色，不能抹去登记、支付服务和政府协调。评估时还应考虑没有手机或被识别系统漏掉的人，不能由一例推出全民覆盖。

11:00–11:38｜主动承认公共服务的必要性

原创英文概述： Stewart limits the claim by acknowledging the continuing need for governance, infrastructure and better schools. Cash is framed as a means for households to use wider development opportunities rather than a replacement for those opportunities.

中文解释： 这是重要的让步：道路通了，家庭仍可能需要资金把货品运到市场；诊所存在，也不表示人人付得起使用成本。现金与公共服务形成互补关系。雅思高分回答应保留这一限制，而非简化成“只要发钱即可”。

11:38–13:14｜从试点倡议走向尊严原则

原创英文概述： The closing appeal asks individuals, major donors and institutions to expand direct giving. Claims about available resources support a proposal for larger demonstrations, while the final ethical argument centres on recipients having authority over their own lives.

中文解释： 11:38 是动员，12:17 转向资金可行性和伦理收束。全国尺度的脱贫在此是希望进一步展示的目标，不能写成已被证明的结果。关于全球援助总额足够的说法取决于测算口径和执行条件；结尾的核心价值是平等与尊严。13:07 致谢、13:09 掌声，至播放器13:14结束；片尾没有新增论点。

三、深入语言分析

以下完整英文例句均为原创；重点是具体机制、准确搭配和谨慎结论。

1. What … is …：辨认真正约束

原创例句： What prevents some traders from expanding is a lack of affordable credit, rather than a lack of ambition.

中文：what 从句整体作主语，is 后交代障碍；适合 Task 2 与 Part 3。不要写 What prevents … are a lack；rather than 连接平行名词结构。结论有 some 限定，避免把所有经营困难归于同一原因。

2. although / despite：让步结构不混用

原创例句： Although the programme reached many families, its long-term effects remain uncertain.

原创例句： Despite its wide coverage, the programme still requires careful evaluation.

中文：although 后接完整分句；despite 后接名词或动名词。适合学术写作，口语也可用。不要写 despite it reached，或 although … but …；覆盖面大与长期有效并非同一指标。

3. could have + 过去分词：未实现的过去可能

原创例句： With lower administrative costs, the same budget could have supported several additional community centres.

中文：用 could have supported 回看过去的另一种可能，适合讨论机会成本；不是说当时确实支持了更多中心。不要省略 have，也不要在缺乏成本资料时编造精确倍数。

4. enable someone to / allow someone to

原创例句： A small grant may enable a parent to pay transport costs and attend a training course.

中文：enable 强调具备实现某事的条件，allow 可强调条件或许可；两者都可接宾语 + to do。适合口语、写作。不要写 enable a parent paying；资金可能提供机会，仍不保证完成课程。

5. address poverty / meet basic needs

原创例句： Policies that address poverty should help households meet basic needs while improving access to reliable services.

中文：address 是着手应对，不意味着彻底解决；meet needs 是满足需求。正式写作常用，口语可说 help families afford essentials。不要把 address 当作名词地址，也避免 solve people's needs，通常说 meet their needs。

6. access to：区分名词与动词

原创例句： Residents may have access to a clinic but still be unable to afford the journey there.

原创例句： A transport voucher can help residents access local services.

中文：名词 access 后接 to；动词 access 直接接宾语，不写 access to services 作动词结构。正式写作和 Part 3 均适用。设施存在、交通可达与支付得起须分别考虑。

7. a lump sum / regular payments / a loan

原创例句： A lump sum could cover the initial equipment cost, whereas regular payments might help a household manage recurring bills.

中文：lump sum 为一次性款项；regular payments 为定期付款；loan 通常须偿还，grant 通常为补助而非借款。两种现金安排不能互换，适合政策类写作。不要因资金都来自机构就把 grant 叫 loan。

8. effective / efficient / equitable

原创例句： A scheme may be efficient to administer yet fail to be effective for households with complex needs, so its distribution should also be assessed for equity.

中文：effective 看目标是否实现，efficient 看资源使用，equitable 看分配是否公平。适合 Task 2；口语可拆成三句。廉价不自动代表有效，向所有人发同额资金也不自动等于公平，需说明判断标准。

9. be tailored to / a uniform approach

原创例句： Support tailored to local conditions may work better than a uniform approach when communities face different barriers.

中文：tailored to 后接需求、情境或对象；不是 tailored for doing 的固定替换。适合正式写作；口语可说 support that fits local needs。uniform 是一致的，不天然带贬义，统一规则有时也提高透明度。

10. evidence suggests / prove：限定研究结论

原创例句： Evidence from the pilot suggests that flexible grants can improve short-term stability, but it does not establish how the policy would perform nationwide.

中文：evidence 通常不可数，用 evidence suggests，不写 evidences suggest；某几项研究可用 several studies。suggests 表示支持而未作绝对证明；prove 语气更强。适合学术写作，需交代试点、时期、结果指标。

11. in proportion / in absolute terms

原创例句： The proportion of households receiving emergency support fell, even though their number rose as the city grew.

中文：比例的分母是全部家庭；绝对人数可能因人口增长而上升。适合 Task 1 与政策写作。percentage points 表示百分比之间的差，percent 表示相对变化，二者不能混用；演讲数字不应被当作当代作文事实背诵。

12. complement / substitute for / depend on

原创例句： Household grants can complement public transport investment, but their usefulness may depend on whether a reliable service exists.

原创例句： Private purchases cannot always substitute for collective infrastructure.

中文：complement 为补充、相配合，区别于 compliment（赞美）；动词 substitute for 为替代，depend on 为取决于。适合 Part 3 和 Task 2。用具体公共服务解释互补，比空泛地说两者都重要更有说服力。

四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

论证路线： 紧迫问题与希望 → 内部从业者的反思 → 低效项目故事 → 挑战默认培训观念 → 现金项目反例 → 研究转述 → 四个机制 → 技术送达 → 公共服务限制 → 规模化与尊严倡议。

例子怎样服务论点： 学校设施故事让预算机会成本可见；村庄故事解释讲者为什么改变判断；杂货店与不同家庭需求说明机制；手机支付与灾前发款说明执行渠道。例子不只是装饰，每换一个例子，都应追问它解决了哪个疑问。

证据分层： 职业履历和项目见闻是个人经历，能提供线索但不能代表总体。随机试验部分是对研究的转述，方法上比单一见闻更有比较依据，但本篇没有独立核验论文。四个原因是讲者的机制解释，规模化脱贫是倡议目标，尊严与自主权是价值理由。不要把这些层级统写成“科学证明发钱能解决所有贫困”。

衔接练习： 可用原创提示串起一段话：A common assumption is … / One contrasting example is … / A possible explanation is … / This does not remove the need for …。先写清逻辑再选连接词，不必每句都用显眼标记；对照结构用于比较，因果结构则需要说明中间机制。

跟读练习建议（未核验讲者声音）： 选择 08:02 节点后约 12–15 秒，先实际播放两遍，再标出你听到的信息重心、意群和停顿。接着用自己的句子练习：A shop owner may know the market / but lack the funds / to buy enough stock. 斜线仅为建议分组，不是讲者原有停顿。录音后检查是否把 know、lack 和目的说明表达清楚；不声称讲者在这些位置重读或使用特定语调。

五、练习与参考答案

练习 1｜60 秒复述：为什么资金可能比额外培训更有用？

闭稿回答：一个背景问题、两个机制、一个限制。不使用原稿中的人数、乘数或整句引语。

查看参考答案与解释

Stewart argues that some aid programmes misunderstand the main barrier facing poor households. A trader may already understand the business but lack enough money to buy stock. Cash also lets families respond to different priorities, such as school expenses or housing repairs. These mechanisms help explain why flexible assistance can be useful. However, a grant cannot create a functioning clinic or a reliable road by itself. I would therefore see direct payments as one part of development policy, supported by public services and careful evaluation.

中文解释：先交代问题，再解释资本与需求差异；最后保留公共服务限制。用 may、some 和 can 控制范围，没有把研究转述包装成普遍定律。

练习 2｜把搭配迁移到自己的学习情境

分别用 enable someone to、access to、be tailored to 写三句，内容须具体；至少一句包含限制条件。

查看参考答案与解释

A travel grant would enable me to attend a workshop in another city. Students need access to quiet study spaces as well as online resources. Feedback tailored to my writing problems would be useful, provided that I had time to revise my essays afterwards.

中文解释：第一句宾语后接不定式，第二句名词 access 后接 to，第三句 tailored to 接问题；provided that 引入条件，说明反馈还需要复写行动配合。

练习 3｜Part 3：援助应统一提供物资，还是让家庭选择用途？

用约 45–60 秒提出倾向、解释机制、承认另一方案的适用条件。使用 complement 或 depend on，避免绝对化。

查看参考答案与解释

I generally favour giving households some choice because their priorities can differ considerably. One family may need transport to work, while another needs to repair a roof. Flexible support can reflect those differences. However, its value depends on what people can actually buy locally. If shops have been destroyed after a disaster, delivering essential supplies may be more useful at first. Over time, cash assistance could complement the restoration of markets and public services. The decision should therefore reflect local conditions rather than a fixed preference for one method.

中文解释：先给立场，再解释差异；灾后供给受损是原创假设情境，不是演讲报告的案例。用 depend on 指出必要条件，用 complement 说明恢复过程中的配合。

  • 造句2分钟：用 access to、enable someone to 和 complement 各造一句，换成教育或交通话题。
  • 口头2分钟：重答练习3，另换一种条件，例如偏远地区缺少稳定供货，并主动限制结论。
  • 核对1分钟：解释比例下降和人数上升为何可同时成立；检查是否将试点效果误说成全国已脱贫。

掌握标准：能解释一个具体机制、辨认一种证据限制，并自然迁移两个表达；不以记住夸张措辞或历史数字作为学习成果。
