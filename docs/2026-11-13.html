二、分段双语内容导读

时间节点取自官方英文字幕，按最接近的整秒标记。最后一段含掌声和主持人补充。

00:00–00:45｜从成年讲者回望船上的孩子

EN: Le introduces herself through a childhood crossing and makes the bond among three generations the thread of the story. The image of an unfinished puzzle gives her a way to move among episodes without pretending that her identity is settled.

中文：开场将台上的成年讲者与船上的四岁女孩连在一起。拼图让后面的叙事可以跳过年份，选择能解释家庭纽带和身份变化的片段。

00:45–01:40｜没有见过的外祖父

EN: Le describes a writer whose hopes for Vietnam collapsed and whose story survived in the family through her grandmother. The episode shows how someone she never met could still shape her sense of duty.

中文：外祖父烧毁作品，是讲者转述的家庭往事。外祖母反复讲述他的经历，让历史变成下一代感受到的责任，也为母亲后来出走的决定提供背景。

01:40–03:18｜母亲的计划与孩子的记忆

EN: The crossing appears first as a decision made by adults who understood the dangers. Le then switches to the details a child retained, including the movement of the boat and the taste of fruit after reaching an offshore platform.

中文：同一趟旅程有两种叙述尺度。母亲知道海上危险并承担决策，孩子记得船的起伏、平台的灯光和获救后尝到的苹果。讲者也点明哪些险情来自他人讲述，哪些细节属于自己的记忆。

03:18–04:52｜在墨尔本重新起步

EN: After time in a refugee camp, the family settles in Footscray, where many neighbours are rebuilding their lives. Her mother's long shifts, training and spending decisions turn the broad idea of starting over into a set of concrete trade-offs.

中文：难民营之后，故事进入墨尔本的工人阶层郊区。母亲打工、学英语、取得信息技术资格，并在家庭预算紧张时优先支付补习费。衣物细节把取舍具体化，排斥性的辱骂则表明定居与获得归属感之间仍有距离。

04:52–06:07｜家里的照料走向社区

EN: Le recalls staying awake to help her mother through nightmares. As her mother starts businesses, she hears about other families' difficulties. The story widens from mutual care at home to community support, and Le's work earns public recognition.

中文：女儿夜里照顾母亲的片段让照料呈现双向性。母亲开店后接触到其他移民家庭的困境，叙事随之转向争取资助和建立服务中心。讲者同时面对学业要求与社区里具体的困境，获奖成为下一段身份变化的起点。

06:07–07:09｜获奖之后的陌生环境

EN: Public attention brings invitations into settings whose social rules Le has never learned. She considers retreating to familiar routines, and her mother draws a parallel between Le's age and her own age during the crossing.

中文：餐具和谈酒这些细节把陌生感落到日常行为上。母亲用同龄时的经历鼓励女儿接住机会，同时保留自己的表达方式。这里的回扣让早先的渡海故事推动眼前的决定。

07:09–09:00｜重新理解局外人的位置

EN: Speaking about neglected social issues opens up new contacts and career possibilities. Le begins to see unfamiliarity as room to question conventions, then illustrates that view with an early team venture and a move to the United States.

中文：讲者从公开谈青年失业、教育和边缘群体，讲到离开法律职业路线。团队没钱、一起喝汤、深夜工作的画面让试错可见。创业和搬迁是她对自己选择的解释，可用来讨论局外人视角如何影响决策。

09:00–09:49｜外祖母用行动挑战规矩

EN: Le returns to her grandmother, who challenged an abusive family situation by taking her husband to court and representing herself. This earlier act gives the audience a family precedent for the willingness to challenge what seems possible.

中文：回到外祖母年轻时，讲者以她亲自打官司并胜诉的经历呼应团队面对质疑时的行动。时间倒回上一代，听众因而能把两段经历放在一起理解。

09:49–10:42｜告别中的照料

EN: Le recounts what she felt when her grandmother died, then describes the family gathering around the coffin. The detail of a hand kept warm by hours of contact gives their closeness a physical form.

中文：悉尼酒店中的告别是讲者对个人体验的叙述。随后转入 Footscray 佛寺，家人围坐讲述往事。握了很久的手让抽象的亲密关系变成一个可感的动作。

10:42–11:59｜把什么交给下一代

EN: Looking ahead to parenthood, Le wonders how children might develop courage while being kept safe. Returning to the boat leaves that question open. After the applause begins, the host points out that Le's mother is in the audience.

中文：讲者借船头、引擎和地平线重新提问。她希望下一代拥有应对未知的能力，同时希望孩子平安。开放式结尾保留两种愿望之间的张力。约 11:46 起主持人补充母亲就在现场。

三、深入语言分析

以下例句均为原创。

  1. Having + 过去分词 · 写作 Task 2 Having completed the induction, new staff can work independently. 表示先完成培训，再独立工作。分词短语与主句共用主语，避免让不执行该动作的名词接在逗号后。
  2. The more …, the more … · 口语 Part 3 The more often newcomers meet their neighbours, the more comfortable they may feel asking for help. 两个比较结构呈现同步变化。用 may 保留推测语气，避免把个人观察写成普遍规律。
  3. What made … was … · 口语 Part 2 What made the move manageable was the support I received from colleagues. 用名词性从句先提出焦点，再解释是什么使搬迁变得可应付。Was 与整个从句搭配。
  4. Allocate resources to … · 写作 Task 2 Local councils could allocate more funding to evening language classes. 用于正式讨论预算分配。Allocate X to Y 指把资源分给某用途或对象，set aside money for Y 强调预先留出款项。
  5. Settle in / settle into · 口语 Part 2 It took me a month to settle in, and a little longer to settle into a regular routine. Settle in 可单独表示逐渐安顿下来，settle into 后接逐渐适应的角色、环境或规律。
  6. A sense of belonging · 口语 Part 3、写作 Task 2 Shared activities can foster a sense of belonging among new residents. Belonging 强调主观归属感，membership 指成员身份。用 among 引出感受到归属的人群。
  7. Face barriers to + 名词或动名词 · 写作 Task 2 Parents who work shifts may face barriers to attending daytime classes. To 是介词，后面接 attending。此处还可用 obstacles to participation，并在下一句具体解释时间冲突。
  8. Draw on experience · 口语 Part 3、写作 Task 2 Mentors can draw on their own experience when helping apprentices handle setbacks. Draw on 表示把积累当作资源来使用。Refer to an experience 表示在叙述中提到某次经历。
  9. Precarious / temporary · 写作 Task 2 A temporary contract can still offer predictable hours, whereas precarious work may leave employees unsure of their next shift. Temporary 说明有时间限制，precarious 指缺乏保障或难以预期。比较就业类型时可分开讨论期限和稳定性。
  10. Had …, … might have … · 口语 Part 3、写作 Task 2 Had the centre closed earlier, some learners might have missed their final assessment. 倒装相当于 If the centre had closed earlier，用来讨论未发生的过去情形。Might have 将后果表述为可能性。

四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

  • 结构：先交代四岁孩子与台上讲者的关系，再用拼图串起家史、逃离、安家、社区工作和职业转向。临近结尾回到外祖母，最后借航行意象把视线移到尚未出生的下一代。
  • 例子的作用：苹果帮助听众从孩子的记忆进入渡海经历。补习与旧衣的取舍具体呈现家庭预算。母亲提醒女儿两人曾处在相近年龄，让过去的决策影响现在的行动。
  • 证据与观点：渡海的部分细节来自家庭转述，苹果和夜间照料属于个人回忆，创业经历支持讲者对自身选择的解释。关于局外人视角的判断来自她的经验。本演讲未用对照研究估计迁徙或社区支持的总体效果。
  • 跟读练习建议：以下任务根据文本设计。回放 06:07 起的一小段，选一句讲陌生场合的台词，先划分两个意群，再对照音频记录实际停顿。随后用原创句 I knew the subject well / but I was unfamiliar with the setting. 练习在第二意群突出 unfamiliar。最后回放 10:42 附近，比较你如何读陈述与开放式问句。

五、练习与参考答案

练习 1｜45 秒复述

用三个节点解释讲者如何从抵达墨尔本走到参与社区工作。选一个生活细节，并交代它在叙事中的作用。

查看参考答案与解释

After arriving in Melbourne, Le's family settled in a neighbourhood where many residents had recently moved from abroad. Her mother combined paid work with further study and found room in a tight budget for tutoring. That detail shows how much effort went into rebuilding daily life. Le later encountered families who needed support and became involved in community work while studying law.

中文提示：依次说地点、家庭取舍和社区参与。用 That detail shows … 从事件切换到细节的作用。

练习 2｜改写成正式论点

用 allocate … to … 和 barriers to … 各写一句。主题是让轮班工人更容易参加社区课程。

查看参考答案与解释

The council could allocate part of its training budget to weekend classes. A more flexible timetable could reduce barriers to participation for workers on rotating shifts.

中文提示：第一句给出预算用途，第二句解释时段调整可能解决的障碍。两个 to 都是介词。

练习 3｜雅思口语 Part 3

回答 How can local communities help newcomers feel at home? 给出一个具体措施，说明它对哪类人有效，再补充一个实施条件。

查看参考答案与解释

Libraries could run small conversation groups where newcomers and long-term residents meet regularly. This would be especially helpful for people who rarely use the local language outside work, because they could get to know the same people over several weeks. To make that possible, libraries would need to offer sessions at times that suit people with different work schedules.

中文提示：回答从设施和活动入手，说明重复见面如何帮助建立关系，再用开放时段补充实施条件。
