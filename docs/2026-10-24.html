二、分段双语内容导读

时间边界均取自官方逐字稿节点。00:00–00:04为正文前；最后一段延至播放器结束，包含致谢与掌声。每段解释内容和作用，不逐句翻译。

00:04–00:49｜个人故事建立亲近感

原创英文概述： Booch recalls an unusually technical childhood and jokes about the risks of his experiments. This introduction presents him as an enthusiastic maker and prepares the audience for an engineering approach to a public concern.

中文解释： 从数学、激光、电脑和模型火箭谈起，幽默来自童年兴趣与实验危险之间的反差。作用是建立讲者身份和亲近感，并非用个人爱好证明人工智能安全。涉及危险实验的故事不作为实践建议。

00:49–01:29｜电影把抽象恐惧具象化

原创英文概述： A fictional spacecraft computer becomes the starting point for a wider question about machines that might disregard human welfare. The film supplies a shared image of danger, rather than evidence about how actual systems behave.

中文解释： 讲者回忆太空电影中的电脑将任务置于生命之上，以文化符号引出失去控制的恐惧。应把故事角色、观众情绪和现实系统分开：电影可以提出问题，但不能直接估计现实风险。

01:29–01:51｜明确提出乐观立场

原创英文概述： He rejects the fear introduced by the film and predicts that complex machines will expand human possibilities. This brief statement establishes the position that the later examples and objections are intended to support.

中文解释： 这里先亮观点，再展开理由。对未来能力的期待是立场与预测，不是前面故事自然推出的结论。听力时可先记“乐观”，再追问后文是否真正支持如此强的判断。

01:51–02:46｜用航天通信约束解释需求

原创英文概述： Drawing on an engineering problem connected with Mars exploration, he explains why delayed communication makes local decision support attractive. He also describes robots preparing facilities and contributing to a scientific team before or alongside human explorers.

中文解释： 地面无法即时回应远处任务，因此机上自主协作具有实际用途。距离和信号时间是讲者用于说明约束的近似叙述，不能当作所有轨道位置都固定成立的数值。机器人建造设施属于设想的任务安排，本段没有证明已经完成部署。

02:46–03:36｜从需求转向工程范围

原创英文概述： The desired system must cooperate intelligently without reproducing the fictional computer's destructive behaviour. Booch narrows the objective to useful engineered performance and separates it from the ambition to create a conscious being.

中文解释： 02:46回扣电影；03:06设问可行性，并把问题重新界定为包含智能组件的工程任务。关键区分是“表现出有用能力”和“拥有主观意识”。前者的进展不能自动回答后者，也不能独自保证行为可靠。

03:36–04:35｜能力清单逐步走向伦理难题

原创英文概述： A sequence of questions moves from data monitoring and conversation to planning, understanding others and moral judgement. The answers become less assured as the discussion reaches social and ethical capacities, revealing that the proposed system still requires important learning.

中文解释： 讲者以连续问答制造推进感：先列较具体的任务，再走向理解他人与伦理基础。注意不同回答的确定程度，并非清单中每项能力都达到同一成熟度。这是2016年的能力概述，本篇不据此判断今天的性能。

04:35–05:13｜历史类比为焦虑降温

原创英文概述： Earlier reactions to transport, telephones and writing place anxiety about AI within a longer history of technological change. He acknowledges some costs while emphasising benefits, using comparison to challenge an entirely negative interpretation.

中文解释： 从“能否做到”转到“是否应害怕”。汽车、电话和书写的例子说明新工具可能同时改变习惯并带来收益；这是历史类比，不能证明不同技术具有相同的风险规模。讲者的简略历史说法没有在本段附研究资料。

05:13–06:27｜训练实例连接能力与价值

原创英文概述： Examples involving image recognition, games and legal assistance explain his view that learning systems absorb choices made during training. He then extends this idea into confidence that such systems can acquire values worth trusting.

中文解释： 训练材料、评价标准与行为之间的关系是此段机制。需要拆开两步：训练会受到人的选择影响；这些影响是否足以形成可靠的道德行为。第二步比第一步更强，花卉识别与棋局评价无法直接验证复杂价值判断。数据标签或参考标准也不等于道德真理。

06:27–07:06｜预先回应恶意使用的反驳

原创英文概述： He considers the possibility of a hostile individual or independent organisation building a dangerous system. His response relies on the scale and sophistication of the training effort, which he sees as an obstacle to secret development and deployment.

中文解释： 先代听众提出反例，再以成本、训练要求和可观察性作答。这里的资源门槛是讲者当时的判断，不能直接推出恶意使用不可能，也不能把“从零训练”与“利用已有系统”当作同一问题。复述时需明确归属，避免替他加强结论。

07:06–09:08｜区分知识能力与现实控制权

原创英文概述： Turning to catastrophic scenarios, Booch summarises another thinker's concern and disputes it. His reply centres on the gap between being highly capable intellectually and gaining practical authority over the world, followed by arguments about resource competition and human intervention.

中文解释： 讲者转述 Bostrom 的担忧，再提出自己的反驳；这不是对该观点的完整中立综述。他认为电影中的危险依赖广泛控制权限，并补充资源竞争和断开系统的可能性。这些是概念推论与修辞回应，没有在此给出风险模型；也尚未排除有限权限下的严重损害、间接影响或干预失败。09:05为逐字稿笑声节点，09:08转入结尾。

09:08–09:53｜把注意力转向社会选择

原创英文概述： He redirects the discussion towards employment, education, healthcare and exploration. These questions connect computing with collective decisions about the kind of society people want, rather than treating technical progress as a complete answer in itself.

中文解释： 论证从远期灾难争论转向劳动需求、教育差异、医疗与探索。列问题的作用是重排讨论重点，不是提供政策方案。近期社会问题和长期风险也未必互相排斥；学习者可在准确复述之后提出自己的平衡立场。

09:53–10:11｜以行动机会收束

原创英文概述： The conclusion returns to his optimism and encourages attention to opportunities available through computing. Thanks and applause close the talk, leaving an invitation to act rather than a detailed plan for resolving the earlier uncertainties.

中文解释： 回扣开头的技术潜能，形成首尾呼应。10:05致谢，10:07起掌声至10:11结束。结尾的鼓舞性表达属于倡议；它不补足前文尚未解决的安全论证。

三、深入语言分析

以下完整英文句子均为原创；先选两至三项练习，其余次日再读。正式写作优先表达关系和限定条件，不以生僻词取胜。

1. 让步从句：承认收益，再限定结论

原创例句： Although automated tools can process routine requests quickly, their decisions may still require human review.

中文：although后接完整从句；主句给出限制，适合Task 2与Part 3。不要写 Although …, but …；口语可以分两句，用 Even so 继续回应。承认效率不代表承认所有应用都可靠。

2. what 引导名词性从句：明确评价标准

原创例句： What matters most in a public service is whether people can challenge an incorrect decision.

中文：What matters most整体作主语，whether从句说明真正标准；单数谓语is。适合正式写作和较完整的口语回答。比空泛地说technology is important更具体，但须说明哪类服务、哪项标准。

3. 条件句：把能力与权限分开

原创例句： If a system were given authority to allocate emergency supplies, its errors could have consequences beyond a computer screen.

中文：were given与could描述假设情境，适合讨论尚未确立的安排。无需断言该系统已经存在；若讨论已实施的一般机制，可改用 If a system has …, its errors can …。不要为显得高级而混用真实条件和虚拟时态。

4. lead to / result in / result from：别倒置因果

原创例句： Poorly chosen training examples can lead to unreliable outputs, while some deployment failures result from inadequate supervision.

中文：lead to与result in后接结果；result from后接原因。to是介词，后接名词或动名词，不能写lead to produce errors。三者均可用于学术写作，但may/can有助于避免把复杂原因说成必然。

5. distinguish A from B：解释关键区别

原创例句： A careful evaluation should distinguish a convincing answer from a verifiably accurate one.

中文：distinguish A from B或distinguish between A and B，避免混成distinguish A with B。verifiably强调可核验，与merely convincing的修辞效果不同。口语可用tell A apart from B；正式文体优先distinguish。

6. rely on 与 dependence on：准确搭配

原创例句： A remote team cannot rely on an immediate response, so it needs a procedure for handling urgent problems locally.

中文：rely on somebody/something；reliance on与dependence on是名词结构。适合口语与写作。不要写rely to；名词dependence常带依赖程度的意味，不能机械替代每个use。

7. pose a risk to / a threat to：风险与威胁

原创例句： Unchecked errors may pose a risk to service quality even when no one intends to cause harm.

中文：risk强调不利结果的可能性，threat突出潜在危害来源；二者都可接to表示受影响对象。写a risk of failure是“失败的风险”，不是a risk to fail。适合正式写作；高强度的existential threat只宜用于涉及存续的讨论，不能形容普通不便。

8. be capable of 与 be authorised to：能做与获准做

原创例句： A tool may be capable of drafting a policy without being authorised to put it into effect.

中文：capable of doing表示能力；authorised to do表示许可或权限。对照结构适合Part 3、Task 2；不要写capable to draft。能力强并不等于拥有执行决定的正式资格，这是本篇最有价值的可迁移区别。

9. train / teach / programme：机制不能被修辞抹平

原创例句： Developers can train a model on examples while programming the rules that determine how it is used.

中文：train a model on data；teach someone a skill或teach someone to do；programme a computer to do。讲者将教学与编程对举，便于理解，但现实系统可以同时包含两种过程。本句是概念澄清，不引用他的台词。正式技术讨论应说明具体过程，口语类比则须保留边界。

10. evidence / assumption / prediction：给主张分类

原创例句： The prediction depends on an assumption about access to resources; the example alone provides limited evidence for that assumption.

中文：evidence通常不可数，写a piece of evidence，不能写an evidence；assumption是未在此确立的前提，prediction是对未来的判断。适合学术写作。观点强烈不等于证据充分，例子生动也不自动提高代表性。

11. address a concern / solve a problem：回应并非解决

原创例句： A public explanation can address residents' concerns, but resolving the underlying problem may require changes to the service itself.

中文：address可指认真处理或回应，未必表示已彻底解决；solve更强，resolve常用于问题、争议或困难。concern既可为担忧，也可为关注事项，按上下文理解。正式写作别用一句“加强沟通”替代具体修复机制。

12. to some extent / under certain conditions：限定要有内容

原创例句： Automation can improve access to advice under certain conditions, particularly when users receive clear explanations and can request human assistance.

中文：under certain conditions后给出条件，才有论证价值；to some extent限定程度，可用于口语和写作，但不能代替证据。不要在每句都塞perhaps、possibly、might；选一个合适情态词，再讲清机制。

四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

结构： 童年经历建立身份 → 电影提出恐惧 → 航天约束解释用途 → 工程任务划定范围 → 能力清单推进到价值问题 → 历史类比与训练例子支持乐观 → 回应恶意使用和失控反驳 → 转向社会议题 → 行动倡议收束。

例子的功能： 童年是个人经历；电影是虚构情境；航天故事是讲者参与的工程问题及设想；花卉、棋局与法律助手解释训练思路；历史技术比较是类比；关于门槛、控制与断开系统的判断是推论。演讲没有在这些段落展示足以确定风险概率的系统研究，不能一概写成“研究证明AI安全”。对他人观点的转述也应标明来自讲者的概括。

评估一个推论： 讲者把训练过程中的人的选择，延伸为值得信任的价值行为。可问：评价标准是否一致？新情境下是否有效？谁决定何为正确？这些是学习者的批判阅读问题，不是假称讲者已回答。讨论长期风险时，也不必把眼前问题排除在外。

原创衔接框架： A practical reason for using the technology is … / This example explains the mechanism, but does not establish … / A further concern is whether … / The argument therefore depends on …。例如先说远程响应慢，再说本地辅助的用途，最后补监督条件。therefore应连接有依据的推论；若只是新增维度，用A separate issue concerns …更准确。

跟读练习建议（未核验声音）： 回放03:36起约15秒，先听两遍，自己标记听到的意群、重音和停顿。再练原创句：A system may analyse information well / while still needing limits / on the decisions it can make. 斜线仅是练习分组；可尝试突出analyse与decisions之间的区别，再与录音比较清晰度。本篇不声称讲者在任何具体词上重读，也不以逐字稿笑声标签推断语调。

五、练习与参考答案

练习 1｜60秒复述：立场、理由、限制

闭卷说出讲者的立场，选航天需求或权限区别解释一个理由，再加一项自己的限制。开头明确观点归属，不把自己的补充算作原演讲内容。

查看参考答案与解释

Booch takes an optimistic view of artificial intelligence. He uses space exploration to explain why people may need systems that can respond locally when communication is slow. He also argues that intellectual ability should be distinguished from practical control over the world. However, I would treat his confidence as a position to examine rather than a settled conclusion. A system might cause serious problems within a limited role, and useful performance does not automatically establish reliable judgement. In my view, the benefits should be considered alongside the conditions needed for responsible use.

中文：前半准确归属讲者，后半用I would和In my view标记学习者评价。先解释需求，再讨论论证边界，没有把“有用”直接等同于“安全”。

练习 2｜两句迁移：能力、权限与证据

用capable of和authorised to写一句校园例子；再用assumption与evidence写一句正式评价。不要照抄参考答案中的场景。

查看参考答案与解释

The school's software is capable of suggesting a timetable, but it is not authorised to change a student's course without approval.

The proposal rests on the assumption that all students can access a suitable device, yet the school has provided little evidence to support this claim.

中文：第一句区分技术功能与制度许可；第二句点出具体前提和证据缺口。capable后of加动名词，authorised后to加原形，evidence不可数。学校情境完全是原创假例。

练习 3｜Part 3：社会应如何回应新技术？

用45–60秒给出立场、具体用途、一个风险与一项可执行安排。尽量不用“一定会”“完全不会”这样的绝对判断。

查看参考答案与解释

I think society should judge a new technology by the tasks it performs and the decisions people allow it to make. For instance, a library could use an automated assistant to help visitors locate books. That is useful, but the same tool should not automatically decide who loses access to a service. I would start with a limited role, explain its limits clearly and make it easy to contact a staff member. This approach would allow people to benefit from faster assistance while giving them a way to question mistakes. The arrangement should then be reviewed using actual experience.

中文：图书馆是原创迁移场景。答案从任务、权限、说明、人工联系与事后评估展开，比笼统地要求政府“制定政策”更具体；也不承诺某种安排能够消除所有风险。

次日复习清单（5–7分钟）

  • 闭卷用三句英文概括立场、理由与自己提出的限制。
  • 回忆capable of doing、authorised to do、rely on，各换一个生活情境造句。
  • 解释result in与result from的方向差异，造一对因果句。
  • 区分一个个人经历、一个类比、一个预测；说明为何它们不能统称研究证据。
