## 二、分段双语内容导读

### 00:04–00:34｜从一个实验谜题开始

**原创英文概述：** A vacuum tube and an applied voltage introduce an unexplained laboratory phenomenon. Rather than starting with a promised product, Sheehy begins with a question about what the observed rays consist of.

**中文解释：** 开头把抽象的基础研究变成“看见现象但不知原因”的问题。真空管演示帮助观众理解研究从疑问出发，尚无确定应用目标；这为后文讨论研究价值做铺垫。无需背诵仪器术语，先辨认“现象—问题”的关系。

### 00:34–01:27｜电子发现与当时难见的用途

**原创英文概述：** Experiments with electric and magnetic effects lead to the identification of a much smaller constituent of matter. A historical anecdote then highlights the gap between recognising a discovery and anticipating its practical uses.

**中文解释：** 讲者介绍 J. J. Thomson 与电子，再用祝酒轶事制造反差：重要发现未必立刻有商业用途。官方字幕将姓名写作 Thompson，本篇采用通常的 Thomson 拼写。脚注指出轶事可能出自实验室其他成员，不能当作已确定的个人原话；不需要引用它来支撑作文。

### 01:27–02:43｜提出主张，把科学联系到日常生活

**原创英文概述：** Sheehy argues that the wish to understand nature can produce unexpected technological consequences. Everyday contact with a chair and the history of television bring an invisible scientific idea into familiar experience, preparing the audience for wider applications.

**中文解释：** 此处明确中心主张，再从座椅的微观相互作用讲到旧式电视机。前者是面向公众的物理解释，后者是技术联系；两者使电子不再只是课本名词。关于接触的说法是简化说明，不能据此否认日常意义上的接触。02:41 有笑声标记，02:43 转入下一演示。

### 02:43–03:46｜X 光：用途可能超出最初任务

**原创英文概述：** The demonstration connects energetic electrons with the production of X-rays. Medical imaging illustrates how understanding a physical process can open a route that improving an existing surgical instrument alone might not reveal.

**中文解释：** 从装置机制进入医疗用途，讲者用“改善已有工具”与“理解新的现象”对比，说明两类研究可能产生不同机会。03:07 与 03:11 为官方稿中的设备声标记。脚注明确 X 光先被发现，电子后来才被确认；不能概括成“先发现电子才发现 X 光”。战地影像的时间表也是粗略叙述，不应以某个十五年窗口作精确历史结论。

### 03:46–04:29｜专业身份成为连接点

**原创英文概述：** The tube is reframed as a simple accelerator, allowing Sheehy to introduce her own field. Her work connects questions about particle behaviour with equipment that has practical uses, making the relationship between exploration and engineering more concrete.

### 04:29–05:05｜理论预测到医疗检测

## 三、深入语言分析

以下完整英文例句均为原创。以准确解释因果和限制条件为优先，不堆砌技术名词。

### 1. 过去进行时与一般过去时：研究背景和发现

**原创例句：** The team was investigating an unusual signal when an unexpected pattern appeared in the data.

中文：was investigating 提供持续背景，appeared 引入新事件，适合 Part 2 描述项目，也适合正式的研究叙述。不要因为两件事都在过去就全部改成过去进行时；出现的模式也不自动等于发现的原因。

### 2. 非限定性定语从句：补充信息而不挤满主句

**原创例句：** The prototype, which was originally designed for laboratory use, later became useful in manufacturing.

中文：逗号中的 which 从句补充最初用途，主线是原型后来发挥作用。适合 Task 2，口语可拆成两句。非限定性从句不能用 that，也不要把 which was 错写成 which it was。

### 3. 过去反事实：没有某条件，结果可能不同

**原创例句：** Without sustained funding, the team might not have developed the instrument that enabled later experiments.

中文：might not have + 过去分词表达对过去另一种可能的推测；might 比绝对断言更谨慎。适合 Task 2 解释研究条件。不要写 might not developed；现实中是否确实缺少其他路径，仍需证据，语法正确不保证反事实成立。

### 4. It is … that …：突出连接机制

**原创例句：** It is the exchange of ideas between disciplines that helps researchers recognise unexpected applications.

中文：强调句把焦点放在跨学科交流，而非某一项神奇发明。适合正式写作，口语直接说 Ideas from different fields can help us find new uses 更自然。不要同时加入多余的 it，或每段都套用强调结构。

### 5. curiosity about / be curious about

**原创例句：** Curiosity about how materials behave can lead students to ask more precise scientific questions.

中文：名词 curiosity 与形容词 curious 均常接 about；how 从句用陈述语序，不写 about how do materials behave。适合口语和写作。precise 表示问题界定更准确，不意味着答案已确定。

### 6. basic research / applied research

**原创例句：** Basic research seeks deeper understanding, while applied research usually addresses a more specific practical problem; the two can inform each other.

中文：basic 在此是基础性，不是简单或低水平；applied 是面向具体应用。适合 Task 2 与 Part 3。二者可能交叉，避免写成绝不重叠的二选一，也不要把 basic research 译成初级研究。research 通常不可数，用 a research project 或 several studies，不写 a research。

### 7. make a case for / evidence for

**原创例句：** These examples make a case for long-term funding, but they do not provide evidence that every project will produce a marketable invention.

中文：make a case for 是提出支持理由，evidence for 强调支持某命题的证据。适合正式讨论与 Part 3；前者不等于 prove。不要写 make a case of funding 来表达支持资助；every 会显著扩大结论范围。

### 8. lead to / enable / contribute to

**原创例句：** A new measurement technique can enable more accurate experiments and contribute to improvements in production.

中文：enable 后接宾语，或 enable someone to do；contribute to 与 lead to 后接名词或动名词，to 在此是介词。lead to 的结果联系通常比 contribute to 更直接；描述众多因素之一时选后者更稳妥。适合 Task 2，避免 contribute to improve 和 enable researchers developing。

### 9. discovery / invention / innovation

**原创例句：** The discovery of a physical effect may inspire the invention of a device, while innovation can involve making that device affordable and widely usable.

中文：discovery 发现原本存在的现象；invention 发明装置或方法；innovation 可包括新方法及其应用改进。适合科技话题口语与写作。不能把发现自然粒子写成 invent a particle；从科学到推广也不是一步完成。

### 10. invest in / allocate … to …

**原创例句：** Governments can invest in scientific training while allocating some research funds to projects with uncertain commercial outcomes.

中文：invest in 接领域、人才或项目；allocate funds to 接资源去向，allocate funds for 强调用途。适合 Task 2。不要写 invest money on science；uncertain 不等于毫无价值，也不表示资金无需问责。口语可用 spend more on training，但 spend 与 invest 的介词不同。

### 11. practical / practicable / theoretical

**原创例句：** A theoretical proposal may be scientifically valuable before engineers can turn it into a practicable design with a clear practical benefit.

中文：theoretical 属于理论层面；practicable 是可实行、做得成，较正式；practical benefit 是实际益处。适合写作，日常口语用 workable design 更直白。理论性不等于不真实，practical 也不能在所有语境替换 practicable。

### 12. may / will；in the long term / long-term

**原创例句：** Exploratory work may benefit society in the long term, but long-term support should still include transparent review.

中文：may 留下结果不确定性，will 容易把潜力变成保证。in the long term 是状语；long-term 放在名词前作形容词。适合 Task 2 与 Part 3；不写 in long-term。后半句给出问责条件，避免把支持研究写成无限支出。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

**论证路线：** 实验谜题 → 电子与用途难预测 → 中心主张 → X 光及个人专业 → PET、放疗、芯片案例 → 加速器带来的能力积累 → 大型装置与规模感 → 支持开放探索的倡议。

**例子与证据层级：** 仪器演示帮助理解机制；历史叙述和应用联系构成案例论证；职业经历说明讲者的专业视角；关于研究的兴奋属于个人评价；文学引语提供修辞力量；资助与耐心是规范性主张。本演讲不是随机实验或系统综述。所选成功案例能反驳“没有眼前用途就必然无价值”，却无法证明所有基础研究都比应用研究更划算。病例比例、装置数量与时间线均应保留演讲年代和官方脚注限定。

**衔接如何起作用：** 起初用未知现象吸引注意，再把陌生概念接到座椅和电视；随后用不同领域的案例扩展范围，而非反复重复同一个好处；06:10 后从产品列表提升到能力积累，结尾再把历史转成未来行动。雅思写作可采用“主张—一个机制—一个具体例子—适用限制”，不必列出四五项专业技术。

**原创衔接示例：** Public research can create tools that private firms later use. For example, a more accurate instrument may improve the testing of industrial materials. However, this possibility supports a balanced funding strategy rather than a promise that every experiment will generate profit.

中文：第一句提出机制，第二句提供假设性例子，第三句限制推论。这是原创迁移情景，不是演讲中已验证的企业案例。比较基础与应用研究时，也应解释应用项目如何解决紧迫问题。

**跟读练习建议，未核验声音特征：** 在 03:46–04:29 内自选约 10–12 秒，先听两遍，再依据真实声音标注信息焦点和停顿，录音比较一次。字幕换行与笑声标签都不能告诉你每句话的实际重音位置；不要预先声称讲者在哪个词上升调。

**原创替换句：** Research can begin with a question / rather than a product / and still create useful possibilities.

斜线仅为练习分组建议。先清楚表达 question 与 product 的对照，再自行尝试突出 useful possibilities；这不是对讲者语音的记录。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习 1｜一分钟复述：解释路径，保留限制

用 70–95 词说明中心观点、两个应用领域、加速器的连接作用，并说明案例不能证明什么。可看关键词，不照读原稿。

**原创参考答案：**

Sheehy argues that research motivated by questions can create benefits that are difficult to predict at the start. Her examples connect investigations of particles with medical imaging and semiconductor manufacturing. Accelerators matter because they help researchers understand and control processes at very small scales. The talk therefore supports giving scientists time and resources to explore unfamiliar problems. However, its successful examples do not show that every project will produce a useful invention. A sensible funding policy would support exploration while also reviewing costs and progress.

中文解释：先讲观点，再以两个领域支撑，用能力积累解释它们之间的共同点；最后区分成功案例与每个项目的保证。这里的政策平衡是原创评价，不冒充讲者提出的完整制度方案。

### 练习 2｜三句迁移：从科研转到大学学习

分别使用 curiosity about、enable someone to、contribute to + 动名词，说明学生探索不同课程的潜在价值；至少用一次 may 或 can。

**原创参考答案：**

Curiosity about urban life encouraged me to take a course outside my main subject. Learning basic statistics enabled me to examine local transport data more carefully. Such experiences may contribute to making better-informed academic choices.

中文解释：三句依次是动机、能力与可能结果。注意 enabled me to examine 的不定式，以及 contribute to making 的动名词。better-informed 说明信息更充分，不暗示选择一定正确。

### 练习 3｜Part 3：政府是否应只资助有即时用途的研究？

作答约 45–60 秒，给出明确立场、一个机制、一个限制及平衡做法。避免绝对词，也不依赖演讲中的历史数字。

**原创参考答案：**

I would not limit public funding to projects with an immediate use. Some scientific questions help us develop methods that later become useful in other fields, even when researchers cannot identify a commercial product at the beginning. At the same time, governments have urgent responsibilities, including maintaining public services. I would therefore support a mixture of targeted projects and exploratory research, with clear explanations of how money is spent. The aim should be to allow uncertainty without removing accountability.

中文解释：第一句立场清楚，中间说明方法可迁移的机制，再承认公共预算的机会成本。最后提出组合资助与透明使用资金；不靠夸大基础研究收益来表达支持。
