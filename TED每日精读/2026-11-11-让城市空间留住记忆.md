## 三、深入语言分析

以下例句均为原创，可迁移到 IELTS 口语或学术写作。

1. **What matters is whether …｜主语从句与表语从句**

    *What matters is whether residents can still use the square after dark.* 用 **what matters** 设定判断标准，再用 **whether** 引出待判断事项。口语 Part 3 适合用它将抽象讨论转为具体条件；写作中从句用陈述语序。
2. **By + 动名词｜交代手段**

    *By keeping the original paving, the design team made the former market easier to recognise.* 适合 Task 2 解释措施和结果。句首动作的执行者应与主句主语一致，这里保留路面的是设计团队。
3. **Even if / even though｜假设让步与事实让步**

    *Even if the city restores the façade, residents may still need affordable space inside.* *Even though the hall reopened last year, evening classes are still limited.* 前句讨论可能情形，后句承认既有事实。两者都适合 Task 2 补充政策局限。
4. **Make room for + 名词｜为……留出空间**

    *The new exhibition makes room for accounts from former factory workers.* 既可指物理空间，也可指让某些观点进入讨论。口语自然，写作可具体化为 *include accounts from*。
5. **Access to / accessible to｜可获得性**

    *Residents have access to the courtyard, but the upper floor is still inaccessible to wheelchair users.* 名词用 **access to**，形容词用 **accessible to**；作动词时可说 *access the courtyard*。适合 Task 2 指明谁能进入或使用设施。
6. **Preserve / restore / commemorate｜三个动词的对象**

    *The council preserved the original tiles, restored the damaged hall and commemorated the workers who built it.* **Preserve** 指保存现存事物，**restore** 强调修复或恢复，**commemorate** 常用于纪念人或事件。写作中可按行动目标分别选词。
7. **Bring people together / foster interaction｜同义改写与语域**

    *A regular street market brings neighbours together.* *Shared seating may foster interaction among visitors.* 第一种适合口语讲具体例子，第二种适合学术写作，**may** 给未经验证的效果留出余地。
8. **Be rooted in + 名词｜说明根源**

    *The festival is rooted in the neighbourhood's history as a trading port.* **In** 后接传统、经历或历史背景。适合口语 Part 2 介绍地方活动，也可在 Task 2 说明某项传统为何重要。
9. **Evidence / example｜证据与例子**

    *The garden is an example of local cooperation; visitor counts could provide evidence of wider use.* 个案可展示机制，客流统计可用来检验使用规模。学术写作中 **evidence** 通常不可数，可用 *a piece of evidence*；**example** 可数。
10. **Consult someone about something｜说明咨询对象与议题**

     *The planning team consulted local tenants about the proposed changes to the station entrance.* 可用于 Task 2 提出程序性措施。**Consult** 可直接接人，用 **about** 引出议题；口语可改用 *ask residents what they think about …*。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

- **结构**：从共同身份的提问出发，经由花园合作、校园纪念、共享公园、剧院修复、码头遗址逐步拓展。末尾的水面装置重新落到记忆如何通过日常接触得以延续。

- **衔接练习**：在 05:56.948 附近听辨从纪念转入同理心的主题切换。在 07:05.552 附近记录叙述怎样从建成时转到多年后。复述时可用原创过渡 *The project shows what can happen on one site. A broader question is who gets to use public space.*

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习一｜用四句话复述一个案例

选择奥克兰公园，依次说出提案、阻碍、实施与可以得出的有限结论。

**原创参考答案：** Hood's team proposed a park that different groups could use throughout the day. The mayor declined to fund it, so the designers sought support from other organisations. They built a space with facilities such as toilets and picnic tables. Its continued use illustrates the value of shared amenities, while the city's wider housing problems require separate measures.

中文解释：用 *so* 连起拨款受阻与另找支持，用 *while* 区分共享设施的作用和更广泛的住房问题。

### 练习二｜用两种结构改写同一观点

用 *by + 动名词* 和 *even if* 各造一句，说明改造旧车站时怎样让更多人参与。

**原创参考答案：** By inviting former railway workers to contribute photographs, the library could document how the station was used. Even if the building attracts more visitors, the council should check whether nearby residents can afford to attend its events.

中文解释：第一句说明征集照片怎样帮助记录历史；第二句先承认客流可能增加，再用活动费用提出可检验的参与门槛。

### 练习三｜IELTS Speaking Part 3

城市为什么值得保留一些旧建筑？给出一个理由、一个例子和一个条件，尝试讲 45 秒。

**原创参考答案：** Old buildings can help people understand what happened in a neighbourhood before they moved there. For example, a former railway station could become a library with a small exhibition about the people who worked on the line. I'd support the project if residents could help decide how the building was used. That would give the city a way to preserve local history and meet an everyday need.

中文解释：把抽象的历史价值放进旧车站改图书馆的例子，再用 *if* 交代支持条件，让立场更具体。
