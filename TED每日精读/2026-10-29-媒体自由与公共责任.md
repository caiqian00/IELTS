## 三、深入语言分析

以下完整英文例句全部原创。今天先练第1、4、9项，其余分日复习。涉及法律的词仅作语义辨析，不提供法律意见。

### 1. have a responsibility to do：从行为说明责任

**原创例句：** News organisations have a responsibility to correct factual errors promptly and make the corrections easy to find.

中文：a responsibility to后接动词原形；responsibility for后接名词或动名词，如responsibility for checking sources。适合Task 2和Part 3。不要写responsible of；形容词通常用responsible for。具体行动比单写“媒体应该负责”更有说服力。

### 2. even though / even if：事实与假设

**原创例句：** Even though the article includes several interviews, its central claim still needs supporting data.

中文：even though承认已知事实；even if引入假设条件，例如即使未来加入采访，仍需数据。二者都适合口语和写作。主句不要再加but。不能因为出现采访就自动把文章当作研究证据。

### 3. foreseeable / inevitable：可预料不等于必发生

**原创例句：** Publishing an unverified allegation creates a foreseeable risk of reputational harm, although the extent of that harm is uncertain.

中文：foreseeable表示有理由预料，inevitable表示不可避免，后者力度更强。risk of后接名词或动名词；适合正式写作。harm通常不可数，不写many harms来泛指伤害。句子保留结果程度的不确定性。

### 4. be associated with / cause：关联与因果

**原创例句：** Frequent exposure to hostile commentary may be associated with lower trust, but that relationship alone does not establish causation.

中文：be associated with说明关联；cause或lead to表达因果，需要相应依据。适合Task 2和Part 3。with是介词，不写associated to。may只降低确定性，不能凭空补足证据；也须考虑原本不信任的人可能更爱阅读此类内容。

### 5. reinforce / undermine：同一对象上的不同作用

**原创例句：** Transparent corrections can reinforce public trust, whereas repeated evasions may undermine it.

中文：reinforce是加强，undermine是逐渐削弱，均可直接接trust、confidence等名词，不加介词。适合两种语域。不要把reinforce当成必然“改善”：它也能加强偏见或错误观念。whereas适合明确对比两个情况。

### 6. balance A with B：把冲突具体化

**原创例句：** Editors need to balance rapid publication with careful verification, particularly when a claim could affect someone's livelihood.

中文：balance A with B强调兼顾；strike a balance between A and B也自然。适合写作与口语。避免混成balance between A with B。verification指核查过程，和仅仅再次转述一个说法不同；后半句解释为什么速度不能成为唯一标准。

### 7. accountable / responsible / liable：三种责任焦点

**原创例句：** An editor may be responsible for checking a report and accountable to readers for explaining how errors occurred.

中文：responsible for强调任务或行为责任；accountable to强调向谁解释、接受追问，accountable for接需要负责的事项；liable常涉及法律责任或可能遭受某结果，不能随意互换。适合正式写作；Part 3可用have to explain。不要仅凭道德批评写出媒体已承担特定法律责任。

### 8. impartial / accurate：公正与准确分别检查

**原创例句：** A report can contain accurate figures while presenting only one group's interpretation of them.

中文：accurate关注事实、数字等是否准确；impartial关注是否不偏袒。适合两种语域。两者相关但不能互相替代；同时转述两方观点也不自动保证准确。while在本句表达对照关系，后面的presenting与主句主语a report相接。

### 9. while …, … should …：让步后加保障条件

**原创例句：** While independent oversight may improve transparency, any complaints process should protect legitimate criticism and allow decisions to be challenged.

中文：while在此相当于although，先承认潜在收益，再给制度条件。适合Task 2及Part 3。oversight在这里是监督，另一义项是疏漏，要看语境；legitimate criticism是正当批评。不要只写“需要平衡”却不说明如何防止误用。

### 10. the extent to which：把绝对判断改为程度问题

**原创例句：** The extent to which a headline informs readers depends partly on whether it reflects the evidence in the article.

中文：the extent to which引导“在多大程度上”的结构；主语核心extent为单数，所以用depends。适合学术写作。partly承认还有其他因素；不能把a headline is popular直接替换成a headline is informative，受欢迎与信息充分是不同维度。

### 11. reconcile A with B / compromise on：协调与让步

**原创例句：** A newsroom may reconcile commercial pressures with editorial independence by making advertising relationships transparent.

中文：reconcile A with B表示使两者协调；reach a compromise on an issue表示在某议题上达成妥协。适合Task 2与Part 3。compromise on accuracy则可能表示在准确性上让步，含义不同。句中方法只是原创建议，不保证仅靠公开广告关系就能消除所有利益冲突。

### 12. suggest that / demonstrate that：动词决定证据力度

**原创例句：** The interviews suggest that some readers value detailed corrections, but they do not demonstrate that every reader will change their opinion.

中文：suggest that表示提供线索或提示；demonstrate that通常表示较充分地显示。适合学术写作。some与every控制概括范围，不能在结论里悄悄扩大样本。suggest还可表示建议；此处是“提示某种情况”，不是劝读者采取行动。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习 1｜60秒复述：讲清主张与证据限制

闭卷说明中心观点、开头案例的作用和一个反对意见；再说出调查结果不能单独证明什么。

**原创参考答案：**

Puttnam argues that influential media organisations should consider their responsibilities to the public. He begins with a consumer case to explain why avoiding foreseeable harm matters, then applies that idea to the quality of public information. He also recognises concerns about restrictions on expression. His discussion combines professional self-restraint with a limited public role in encouraging reasonable care. However, the survey he describes cannot by itself show that newspaper reading caused particular political attitudes. I see the talk as an argument for responsibility whose practical boundaries still need careful discussion.

中文：先标明观点归属，再说明类比用途；同时保留自律与有限外部作用，最后指出关联和因果的差别。不要把自己赞同的方案写成讲者已经证实的结论。

### 练习 2｜两句迁移：教育与科技

第一句用be associated with描述学习习惯与成绩的关系，不下因果结论；第二句用balance A with B说明学校采用数字工具时的取舍。

**原创参考答案：**

Regular independent reading may be associated with stronger writing skills, although students' prior ability and home support also need to be considered.

Schools should balance the convenience of digital learning tools with the need to protect students' personal information.

中文：第一句提出其他可能影响因素；第二句把平衡双方写成具体目标。with后接名词短语，避免在两个不同结构间生硬并列。这里是原创语言练习，不声称引用了教育研究。

### 练习 3｜Part 3：媒体应该为不准确的信息负责吗？

用45–60秒给有条件的回答，包括一项实际措施、一项自由保障和一个判断标准。尝试使用responsibility to、accountable to或while一次。

**原创参考答案：**

Yes, particularly when a factual claim could seriously affect someone's reputation or choices. News organisations have a responsibility to check important claims and correct significant errors clearly. However, I would distinguish an honest mistake from repeated carelessness, and I would also separate factual reporting from clearly labelled opinion. While a complaints process could help readers, it should remain independent and allow decisions to be challenged. The aim should be to make organisations accountable to the public while preserving their ability to investigate powerful people. I would judge the process by its transparency and fairness, rather than by whether everyone agrees with a report.

中文：先回答并限定场景，再给核查与更正两项行动，区分错误类型和报道类型，最后提出程序条件。意见也可能包含事实断言，标注为评论并不免除事实核查；此处的区分用于避免把不同意见直接当作事实错误。
