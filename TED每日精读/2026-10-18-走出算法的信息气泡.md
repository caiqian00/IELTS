## 二、分段双语内容导读

### 00:03–00:28｜先问“相关”是否足够

**原创英文概述：** Pariser opens with a reported remark by a technology leader. It sets up a tension between what feels personally relevant and what matters beyond a person's immediate surroundings.

**中文解释：** 开场引用他人说法，把私人兴趣与远方公共事件放在一起比较。作用是提出平台筛选标准的价值问题，不是先展示一项效果研究。听时抓住“与我有关”和“值得公众了解”之间的差别。

### 00:28–01:42｜从联网的期待转到个人困惑

**原创英文概述：** His early hope for a more connected society contrasts with a later experience on social media. He reports that opposing political voices became less visible after the service responded to his clicking habits.

**中文解释：** 讲者先交代乡村成长经历与互联网期待，再讲自己发现保守派朋友内容从信息流消失。他把变化解释为点击偏好影响筛选，而且自己没有主动要求删除这些声音。这是个人观察与机制解释，不能推出所有用户必然有同样经历。

### 01:42–02:30｜把问题延伸到搜索

**原创英文概述：** He argues that search can also vary between users, drawing on an engineer's account of personalisation signals. The difficulty is that individuals normally see only their own results, making differences hard to detect.

**中文解释：** 演讲从单个平台扩展到搜索，转述工程师关于设备、浏览器与位置等信号的说法，并指出信息不对称：你通常不知道别人看到了什么。这里的技术细节属于当时的转述，不是本篇核验的当前产品规格；重点是“差异不易被用户看见”。

### 02:30–03:09｜截图让隐蔽差异可见

**原创英文概述：** Two friends' searches about the same country provide a visual comparison. In his presentation, one first page highlights political unrest while the other does not, making the abstract concern easier to grasp.

**中文解释：** 讲者请朋友搜索埃及，再展示两人的首页差异，把看不见的筛选转为并排可看的案例。这个例子具有说明力，但不是随机抽样或控制实验；不能仅凭两张截图就排除全部其他原因，也不能据此估计影响规模。

### 03:09–03:53｜从行业趋势转向需求差别

**原创英文概述：** He describes personalisation spreading across news services and distinguishes predicted preferences from broader informational needs. A service can become better at anticipating attention without necessarily improving public understanding.

**中文解释：** 此段列出新闻机构，扩大问题范围，并把论点收束到“想看什么”与“需要知道什么”的差别。机构名单和规模描述具有 2011 年语境；复述时应保留归属与时间。第二句英文是对逻辑的原创概括，不是平台效果的新增实证结论。

### 03:53–04:50｜定义概念，再引入娱乐选择

**原创英文概述：** He names the personalised information environment and stresses the lack of visibility and control over exclusions. A reported finding about film queues then introduces the gap between the choices people aspire to make and those they act on immediately.

**中文解释：** 这一段先解释信息气泡：内容因人而异，用户却不完全控制进入与被排除的信息。随后转向 Netflix 队列，为下一段的长期愿望与即时冲动做铺垫。研究描述由讲者转述，演讲在此没有提供足够的方法细节来独立评估其结论。

### 04:50–05:47｜用饮食类比解释失衡

**原创英文概述：** The conflict between immediate enjoyment and longer-term aspirations leads to a food analogy. Pariser argues that filtering based mainly on initial clicks can favour easy consumption over a more varied selection of information.

**中文解释：** 影片选择说明“现在会点”不一定代表“长期最重视”；饮食类比帮助理解娱乐内容与有挑战内容的搭配。类比不是营养学证据，也不意味着娱乐无价值。核心批评是单一信号可能破坏比例，而非所有个性化都应取消。

### 05:47–06:51｜筛选权没有消失，而是换了形式

**原创英文概述：** He challenges the idea that the internet simply removed information intermediaries. Automated selection has taken over part of that role, so he calls for systems to include challenging and socially significant material alongside relevant content.

**中文解释：** 讲者重写“互联网消除把关人”的故事：选择仍在发生，只是更多由算法承担。由此从描述机制转向提出规范要求。关于编辑伦理优于算法的比较是讲者的概括，不能理解为所有传统编辑都公平，或所有算法都没有公共目标。

### 06:51–07:39｜用新闻史类比引出责任

**原创英文概述：** A historical comparison with newspapers connects information filtering to democratic participation. He suggests that the responsibility once associated with editorial practice now needs to become part of software design.

**中文解释：** 讲者借新闻伦理的发展说明筛选者承担公共责任的必要性，并用过去与现在的类比增强改革的可想象性。这是他对历史的压缩解释，不是本篇独立核实的新闻史，也不证明新闻伦理在某一年突然出现。

### 07:39–08:33｜把批评转为设计要求

**原创英文概述：** Addressing technology builders directly, he asks for public responsibility, understandable selection rules and greater user influence. The ending returns to his initial hope that online connection can expand encounters with unfamiliar people and ideas.

**中文解释：** 结尾提出三项方向：公共责任、规则透明、用户控制，再回扣开头对连接世界的期待。它是面向设计者的行动倡议，没有给出完整工程方案或效果评估。08:33 致谢结束正文；08:35 官方稿标注掌声。

## 三、深入语言分析

以下完整英文句子均为原创。练习时优先保证介词、结构和论断力度准确，不用生僻词代替清楚的推理。

### 1. 名词性从句：what … / what …

**原创例句：** What attracts a reader's attention may differ from what helps that reader understand an issue.

中文：两个 what 从句分别充当主语和介词宾语，使“吸引注意”和“帮助理解”形成平行对照。适合 Task 2 和 Part 3；what 已含“所……的内容”，不要再写 the things what。differ from 比 be different 更紧凑，may 保留可能性。

### 2. 让步与范围：although + 从句

**原创例句：** Although personalised recommendations can save time, they may leave some important topics unexplored.

中文：先承认便利，再提出有限度的担忧，适合学术写作。although 后不再加 but；some 与 may 避免把潜在问题说成必然结果。口语可拆成两句，用 but 转折。

### 3. without + 动名词：说明缺少的过程

**原创例句：** A platform should not infer a user's long-term interests without giving that person a chance to correct its assumptions.

中文：without giving 说明推断过程中欠缺的用户机会，适合正式建议；介词后不用 give 原形。infer 是根据迹象推断，不等于 know；assumption 是尚待验证的设定。口语可以说 ask users whether the guess is right。

### 4. enough + 不定式：把抽象要求变成标准

**原创例句：** The explanation should be clear enough for an ordinary user to understand why a story was recommended.

中文：形容词在 enough 前，for 后说明判断对象，适合 Task 2。不要写 enough clear 或 enough for understand。比单说系统应透明更具体：普通用户应能理解推荐理由，但这不等于公开全部源代码。

### 5. expose someone to / be exposed to

**原创例句：** Reading several local newspapers can expose students to perspectives they rarely encounter at home.

中文：主动结构 expose someone to something；被动为 students are exposed to…。to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。这里指接触观点，口语写作均适用；expose 也可表示揭露问题，需根据宾语辨义，不能一律翻译成“曝光”。

### 6. tailor something to / for

**原创例句：** The course is tailored to learners' needs, with optional reading prepared for advanced students.

中文：tailor something to needs 强调按需求调整；tailor something for a group 强调为某群体定制，两种介词搭配都可能成立。适合教育与科技写作；日常口语用 adapt 或 design for 更容易。不要把定制直接等同于提高质量，仍需说明适合什么需求。

### 7. relevance / importance

**原创例句：** A story's relevance to my hobbies does not determine its importance to the wider community.

中文：relevance to 表示与对象的关联，importance to 表示对对象的重要性。两者都是常见不可数抽象名词，适合 Academic 写作；relevant 形容词也接 to。不要因为一条新闻不符合个人兴趣就说它没有公共重要性。

### 8. bias / preference

**原创例句：** A preference for short videos does not by itself demonstrate political bias in a recommendation system.

中文：preference for 是偏好；bias 指偏向，讨论政治立场时常用 bias towards 或 against 某方。适合 Part 3 和写作。by itself 限定证据力度：观看喜好不能单独证明系统有政治偏向。不要把所有个人选择自动命名为偏见。

### 9. evidence / example / proof

**原创例句：** The screenshots provide an example of differing results, but stronger evidence is needed to identify the cause.

中文：example 用来说明，evidence 支持推断，proof 通常暗示更高的确定性。evidence 一般不可数，写 a piece of evidence，不写 an evidence。适合 Task 2；没有足够依据时用 suggests，不用 proves 把案例包装为定论。

### 10. transparency / accountability / control

**原创例句：** Transparency helps users understand a decision, while accountability requires an organisation to explain and answer for its consequences.

中文：透明度强调看得懂，问责强调有责任主体并能要求解释；control 则强调改变设置或结果的能力。三者相关但不互换，适合正式写作。answer for 是为后果负责，不同于 answer a question；单有说明页不代表用户有实际选择权。

### 11. at the expense of / strike a balance between

**原创例句：** News services should strike a balance between convenience and breadth rather than maximise engagement at the expense of public awareness.

中文：between A and B 保持名词平行；at the expense of 表示以牺牲另一目标为代价。适合 Task 2，口语可用 without sacrificing。engagement 在此是互动或参与程度，不是订婚；提出平衡后应补具体措施，不能把词组当作政策本身。

### 12. lead to / result in / be associated with

**原创例句：** Limited exposure to other views may be associated with stronger attitudes, but that association alone does not show that recommendation systems caused the change.

中文：lead to 和 result in 表达结果关系，后接名词或动名词；be associated with 只说关联，适合谨慎讨论研究。不要把相关性自动改写成因果。口语可用 be linked to，但同样需要区分联系与原因；本例为原创方法训练，并非新增研究结论。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

**论证路线：** 相关性的价值问题 → 个人信息流经历 → 搜索机制转述 → 朋友截图 → 行业扩展 → 定义信息气泡 → 长期愿望与即时选择 → 饮食类比 → 把关责任转移 → 新闻史类比 → 透明与控制倡议。

**证据分层：** 成长记忆和信息流变化属于个人经历；搜索截图属于小规模说明案例；工程师说法属于转述；Netflix 段落属于讲者报告的研究观察，本篇未独立核验方法；饮食与新闻史帮助解释和说服，不构成算法效果的因果实验；结尾是价值立场和设计建议。复述可分别用 he recalls、he illustrates、he reports、he proposes，避免全部说成 scientists have proved。

**衔接方式：** 先以个人反差建立问题，再从社交媒体扩展到搜索与新闻；定义概念后用生活类比降低理解难度，最后从机制走到责任。雅思段落可采用“便利—潜在代价—发生机制—可行措施—限制条件”。例如先承认节省时间，再解释单一点击信号为何可能缩窄内容，提出查看多种来源的选项，并说明仍需评估实际使用效果。这套应用是原创迁移，不是演讲已验证的方案。

**跟读练习建议，未核验声音特征：** 从 07:39–08:33 自选约十五秒，先听两遍，再记录自己实际听到的意群和重音，录一次并与视频比较。这里不预先描述讲者的停顿、升调或语速，也不把字幕断行当作声音证据。

**原创替换练习：** A useful news service / saves readers time / while helping them encounter a wider range of views.

斜线是建议分组，不是官方停顿标记。练清 useful、readers、wider 等承载意思的词，再尝试连贯表达；是否重读以及如何连读，要以自己回放后的听音记录为准。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习 1｜一分钟复述：机制、案例与限制

闭卷用 70–100 个英文词概括担忧、一个案例、设计建议和证据边界。不要声称演讲证明所有算法都让人孤立。

**原创参考答案：**

Pariser argues that personalised services can limit the information people encounter without making those exclusions clear. He uses his social media experience and friends' search results to illustrate the concern. The underlying problem is that immediate clicks may not represent everything a person values or needs to know. He therefore calls for greater transparency, user influence and public responsibility in system design. However, the talk was delivered in 2011, and its examples do not establish how widespread the effects are today. Its claims need to be considered within that context.

中文解释：答案从担忧走到机制与措施，再补上时间和样本限制。illustrate 不把案例夸大成证明；can 和 may 让论断与依据相匹配。

### 练习 2｜造句迁移：精确描述推荐功能

写两句英文：第一句用 although 承认便利并指出潜在代价；第二句用 expose … to 提出学校可做的一项具体活动。不要笼统地写“科技不好”。

**原创参考答案：**

Although recommended articles help me find useful material quickly, they may give me fewer opportunities to explore unfamiliar subjects. A weekly comparison of reports from different newspapers could expose students to contrasting interpretations of the same event.

中文解释：第一句限定个人体验和可能性；第二句说明频率、材料和任务。contrasting interpretations 是不同解释，不暗示两种解释必然同样可靠，比较时仍须检查事实依据。

### 练习 3｜IELTS Part 3：应由用户还是平台负责拓宽阅读？

用约一分钟回答：Who should be responsible for making people's news consumption more varied? 给出立场、双方行动和一个限制条件。

**原创参考答案：**

I think the responsibility should be shared, although platforms have more influence over the default choices. They could explain why an article appears and offer a simple option to explore other topics. Readers also need to take an active role, perhaps by checking a second source before forming a strong opinion. However, variety alone does not guarantee accuracy. A service should not recommend an unreliable report merely because it presents a different view. The aim should be a wider selection of credible information, with enough control for users to make informed choices.

中文解释：先分配责任，再分别给行动；结尾区分观点多样与信息可靠，避免“所有相反意见都值得等量展示”的跳跃。例中措施均为原创建议，演讲没有提供其效果测试。

下一主题：文化。
