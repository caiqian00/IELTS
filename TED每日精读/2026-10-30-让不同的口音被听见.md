## 二、分段双语内容导读

### 00:04–00:53｜先让观众进入说不出名字的恐惧

**原创英文概述：** Saleem opens with a recurring nightmare in which an ordinary introduction turns into a public ordeal. The scene establishes the emotional cost of speaking.

**中文解释：** 梦里有人询问姓名，他无法回答，周围的人也开始追问。开头先制造“想表达却无法开口”的体验，再留待后文解释原因。00:43起的多人配音由逐字稿标注，不能把梦境误记为一场真实聚会。

### 00:53–01:50｜展示创作身份与声音的乐趣

**原创英文概述：** He introduces his visual art and the animated characters he voices. These playful examples show that his voice is an important creative resource.

**中文解释：** 动画角色从动物到怪物，说明声音不只是沟通工具，也是他的作品组成部分。之后介绍游戏史教育视频，衔接到网络反馈。角色片段与笑声有官方文本标记；这里并未实际核验其声音效果。

### 01:50–02:59｜肯定逐渐变成对口音的嘲弄

**原创英文概述：** Initial online praise gives way to comments attacking his narration and accent. His response also exposes how casually listeners can misidentify someone's background.

**中文解释：** 他反复刷新评论，先获得认可，随后遇到贬低声音、混淆其国籍口音的留言。由期待转为受伤，让后面的创作停滞有清楚的触发点。演讲呈现的是他选择展示的评论，不是对所有观众态度的统计。

### 02:59–04:01｜网络评论重新触发童年回忆

**原创英文概述：** Attempts to continue the project bring back memories of childhood stuttering. He recalls avoiding classroom participation, phone calls and introductions.

**中文解释：** 他原本想忽略留言继续工作，剪辑时却无法推进。童年躲避电话、自我介绍被取笑等经历解释了这次批评为何格外沉重，也回应开头的姓名梦境。口吃与口音是不同概念，不能混为一谈。

### 04:01–04:48｜剪辑如何帮助他跨过表达门槛

**原创英文概述：** Recording and editing had allowed him to participate despite intense self-consciousness. A demonstration illustrates how much control recorded audio can offer.

**中文解释：** 对他而言，使用自己的声音已是很大的进步；反复录制、挑选和修整给了他创作的可能。04:34起是配音剪辑演示。重点是技术支持如何帮助参与，而不是推断每位口吃者都需要相同方法。

### 04:48–05:16｜从自我否定转向追问社会期待

**原创英文概述：** The comments lead him to stop using his voice, but they also prompt a broader question: how do expectations determine which differences seem acceptable?

**中文解释：** 他曾借修整后的声音获得自我接纳，却因留言再次退缩。这里出现全篇转折：不再只问自己哪里不够好，而是追问人们如何形成对正常的判断。这是讲者对经历的解释，不是已隔离其他变量的研究结论。

### 05:16–06:30｜用颜色故事搭建陌生与不可见的类比

**原创英文概述：** He retells a story about colour language in ancient texts to suggest that familiar categories shape what people notice. The story functions as a bridge to cultural expectations.

**中文解释：** 他从 Homer 等古代文本的颜色描述，推到制造、命名与感知之间的联系。须谨慎区分“文本缺少某类色词”和“人无法看见某种颜色”：前者本身不足以证明后者。本材料未独立核查这一历史与感知主张，不把它当作科学定论；学习重点是识别这个类比如何为社会可见度服务。

### 06:30–06:52｜重新解释对自己口音的反应

**原创英文概述：** Returning to his experience, he considers whether listeners were reacting to an unfamiliar narrator rather than evaluating his personal worth.

**中文解释：** 他认为评论者并不了解自己对声音的不安，主要是在回应不符合预期的口音。这种重构帮助他把个人价值与外界评价分开，但不能据此声称已经掌握每个评论者的真实动机。

### 06:52–07:46｜从个人遭遇扩大到社会偏见

**原创英文概述：** He cites research examples concerning evaluation, academic assistance and hiring, then proposes that preference for familiar people can contribute to unequal treatment.

**中文解释：** 例子涉及写作评价、教授回应和简历回电，意在说明偏见不只表现为公开敌意，也可能来自对熟悉群体的偏爱。这些是讲者在2016年转述的研究；本材料未独立审阅原研究，不能概括所有教授或招聘者，也不能说一切歧视只有这一种原因。

### 07:46–08:41｜儿童故事中的代表性有双重作用

**原创英文概述：** A discussion of children's books connects limited representation with two concerns: minority children may struggle to see possibilities for themselves, while majority children miss opportunities to recognise shared experiences.

**中文解释：** 他转述某图书馆关于2014年及此前一年儿童图书角色的记录，再提出两类影响。少数群体需要看见与自己相关的可能性，多数群体也需要理解共同的生活经验。原统计的收集范围不能自动扩成全部出版物，历史比例不能当作今天的现状；两种影响属于讲者的解释。

### 08:41–09:36｜让个人登台成为论点的一部分

**原创英文概述：** He links visibility to cultural expectations and presents a choice between accepting a narrow standard and challenging it through creative participation. His presence on stage becomes part of the argument.

**中文解释：** 从社会故事回到自己的作品、声音和登台行动，使“增加可见度”不再停留在抽象主张。09:25、09:26有笑声与掌声标记。学习者可以分析“说出观点并现场体现观点”的结构，不必模仿此处粗俗口语。

### 09:36–10:38｜恢复表达，并承认脆弱仍然存在

**原创英文概述：** He describes gradually returning to voice work without claiming that criticism has lost its power. The ending calls for continued challenges to restrictive expectations and closes through his animated characters.

**中文解释：** 角色配音引出他正在重新使用声音；他没有宣称从此不会受伤，而是更清楚坚持表达的意义。10:00再次回到颜色意象，用开头之后建立的类比收束主题。10:23起角色致谢，10:32本人致谢，10:33掌声。结尾有感染力，但类比的回环仍不能替代对相关科学说法的核验。

## 三、深入语言分析

以下完整英文例句均为原创。今天优先主动使用第1、4、9项，其余作为复习库。语域说明指建议使用场景，不是分数保证。

### 1. used to do / would do：过去习惯的两种写法

**原创例句：** I used to avoid group discussions, and I would rehearse a simple introduction before every meeting.

中文：used to可描述过去状态或习惯；would在已建立过去背景后描述反复动作，通常不直接替代过去状态。两者适合Part 2个人叙事；学术写作须有合适的过去语境。不要把used to do与be used to doing混淆，后者表示习惯于。

### 2. self-conscious about / conscious of：不自在与意识到

**原创例句：** Some learners feel self-conscious about their accent even when their message is easy to understand.

中文：self-conscious about常含担心他人眼光；conscious of偏向意识到。适合口语和社会议题写作。注意self-conscious about speaking中about是介词，后接动名词；不能把这类不自在直接等同于能力不足。

### 3. discourage someone from doing：说明阻碍机制

**原创例句：** Repeated ridicule can discourage students from asking questions, even when they need help.

中文：discourage + 人 + from + 动名词，适合Task 2解释课堂环境的后果。encourage则通常用encourage someone to do，结构不同。can表示可能作用，不把所有学生反应写成必然相同。

### 4. be exposed to：接触经验与介词搭配

**原创例句：** Children who are exposed to a wider range of stories may become more familiar with lives unlike their own.

中文：be exposed to中的to为介词，可接名词或动名词，不写exposed with。适合写作和Part 3。exposure既可指接触多样经历，也可指暴露于风险，需看宾语；本例只是可能机制，不是对阅读效果的保证。

### 5. familiar with / familiar to：谁认识谁

**原创例句：** The audience may be familiar with one style of narration, while another style is less familiar to them.

中文：人be familiar with事物；事物be familiar to人。两种语域均适用。familiar表示熟悉，normal涉及常态判断，acceptable涉及能否接受，三者不能偷换；不熟悉并不自动等于不合格。

### 6. not because … but because …：明确替代解释

**原创例句：** A speaker may remain silent not because she lacks ideas but because previous criticism has made participation feel risky.

中文：两个because引导平行的原因从句，适合Task 2与Part 3。使用前要有理由排除或降低第一种解释，不能只靠句式武断判断他人动机。may和previous criticism让范围更清楚。

### 7. bias / discrimination / favouritism：态度、待遇与偏爱

**原创例句：** A preference for familiar candidates may introduce bias into recruitment and, in some cases, contribute to discriminatory decisions.

中文：bias可指判断偏向；discrimination关注不公平区别对待；favouritism指偏爱某人或某群体。适合正式议论。preference for、bias in或bias against搭配焦点不同。不要把没有明显敌意误写成没有不公平后果，也不要把每项偏好都直接定性为歧视。

### 8. representation / representative：可见度与代表性

**原创例句：** Greater representation in children's fiction can broaden the range of lives readers encounter, but one character cannot represent an entire community.

中文：representation在此指某类人在作品中得到呈现；a representative sample指具有代表性的样本。适合文化、教育类Task 2。in children's fiction说明出现的领域；不能因为某群体出现了一个角色，就断定其经历已被完整呈现。

### 9. distinguish A from B：把评价尺度拆开

**原创例句：** Listeners should distinguish an unfamiliar accent from speech that is genuinely difficult to understand.

中文：distinguish A from B，也可用distinguish between A and B；不要混成distinguish A with B。适合口语和写作。accent指发音特征，fluency关注表达的流畅程度，intelligibility关注能否听懂。表达清楚是可练习的目标，不应只以是否像某个群体来判断。

### 10. even though / even if：承认事实与设定条件

**原创例句：** Even though the presenter felt anxious, she completed the discussion and answered several questions.

中文：even though承认实际情况；even if引入假设。适合个人叙事与议论。主句不再加but；“完成讨论”也不意味着焦虑已经消失。写作时保留结果与心理状态的区别。

### 11. challenge an assumption / question a claim：质疑的对象

**原创例句：** A school can challenge the assumption that confident speakers always have the strongest ideas by offering several ways to contribute.

中文：challenge an assumption强调检视预设；question a claim强调追问某个陈述的根据。适合Task 2和Part 3。assumption that后接完整从句；不能写assumption of加完整句。后面的by doing提供具体做法，避免只提出口号。

### 12. suggest / establish：研究转述控制力度

**原创例句：** The comparison suggests that expectations may affect evaluation, but it does not establish how every employer makes a decision.

中文：suggest提供线索，establish表示较充分地确立；两者可接that从句。本句后半使用how从句。适合学术写作。some、may、in this setting等边界词应与实际证据一致，不能用一项研究为所有人下结论。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

**论证主线：** 梦境中的失语 → 创作的快乐 → 网络评价的打击 → 童年经历与剪辑支持 → 追问文化期待 → 颜色类比 → 社会偏见与儿童故事 → 用自己的创作和登台实践主张。它把个人经历、社会解释和行动倡议连在一起。

**例子分别做什么：** 梦境吸引注意并制造理解入口；配音角色让声音的创作价值具体可见；童年回忆解释评论的情绪重量；研究转述扩大议题范围；儿童书籍把抽象偏见接到日常文化产品；结尾角色与颜色意象回环，形成结构上的完整感。

**区分三类依据：** 个人经历可说明讲者为何在意此事，但不能推出所有人的经历相同；评价、学术回应、招聘及图书比例属于研究或记录的转述，本材料未独立复核其设计与适用范围；关于熟悉度如何影响社会期待、个人持续出现如何推动改变，包含机制解释与价值主张。不要把它们全部写成实验已证明的因果链。

**需要保留的疑问：** 颜色类比把“命名或描述”与“感知能力”连接得很强，读者应保留核验意识。即使这个类比存在问题，仍可独立讨论公平评价与文化可见度；但后面的社会主张也应凭自己的证据评估。历史图书比例不可用作2026年的现状。

**迁移衔接：** Part 2可用“一次具体经历—当时反应—后来理解—现在的做法”；Task 2可用“识别评价偏差—解释可能机制—举有限例子—提出可执行措施”。需要反方条件：沟通确实需要清晰度，公平评价也需要把陌生感与真实理解困难分开。

**跟读练习建议（未听音频）：** 回放06:30–06:52，先听再对照官方英文文本，自己标记信息单位与呼吸位置；这里不预先宣称讲者用了何种重音或语调。随后读原创句：Listeners can learn to separate unfamiliarity from a lack of clarity. 尝试分别强调listeners、learn和clarity，体会信息焦点如何改变。目标是清楚传意；不要模仿或夸张口吃，也无需复刻讲者的口音。若想分析实际停顿，应先回放核验，再记录自己的观察。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习1｜60秒复述：由个人经历到公共问题

不看正文，用四步复述：创作与评价、童年影响、文化期待、恢复行动。至少使用一个表示不确定性的词，避免把颜色故事当作定论。

**原创参考答案：**

Saleem explains how criticism of his accent disrupted work that had helped him express himself. The comments brought back difficult childhood experiences, so simply ignoring them was not easy. He then asks whether listeners sometimes confuse unfamiliarity with a lack of ability. His examples about evaluation and children's stories connect personal experience with wider cultural expectations. These examples invite reflection, although they do not justify claims about every person or situation. He gradually returns to voice work because participation matters to him, even though criticism may still hurt.

中文解释：答案保留“个人经历—可能机制—社会范围—行动”的线索；sometimes、may以及限制性让步控制概括力度。没有要求记住演讲的精确措辞，也没有把坚持说成不再脆弱。

### 练习2｜造句与纠错：从感受转为清楚的表达

改写并解释错误：① Students are exposed with different stories. ② I am used to avoid speaking, but now I participate. ③ An accent proves that a speaker cannot communicate well. 再用discourage someone from doing造一个自己的句子。

**原创参考答案：**

1. Students are exposed to different stories through the school library.
2. I used to avoid speaking, but now I participate.
3. An unfamiliar accent does not necessarily prevent effective communication.
4. Public mockery can discourage learners from sharing useful ideas.

中文解释：①改为to并补充接触渠道；②句意是过去习惯，使用used to do，若表示现在习惯才用be used to doing；③拆开口音与沟通能力，避免无根据的必然判断；④from后用动名词。第三句并不意味着任何表达都无需改进清晰度。

### 练习3｜Part 3：学校如何让更多学生愿意表达？

用45–60秒回答。包含一个具体方法、一个可能机制和一个限制条件，使用be familiar with或distinguish A from B。

**原创参考答案：**

Schools could give students a short preparation period before group discussions and include stories featuring a wider range of communities. Preparation may reduce the pressure of responding immediately, while varied stories can help classmates become familiar with experiences beyond their own. Teachers should also distinguish a student's ideas from the confidence of their delivery. However, inclusion does not mean avoiding feedback. Students still need specific guidance on making their message clear, and teachers should check whether quieter pupils actually find the new arrangements helpful.

中文解释：方法包括准备时间、故事选择和评价尺度，机制用may控制力度。结尾补充反馈与实际成效检查，使答案超越“要包容”的抽象表态。这些措施是学习者的原创延伸，不是演讲验证过的干预方案。
