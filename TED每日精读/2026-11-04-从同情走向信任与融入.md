## 三、深入语言分析

以下均为原创例句。学会准确说明关系，比生硬堆叠高级词更有助于7.5分表达。

### 1. not until：强调转折的发生条件

**原创例句：** I did not feel part of the neighbourhood until I joined a local team.

**原创例句：** Not until I joined a local team did I feel part of the neighbourhood.

中文：第一句适合口语经历；第二句强调转折，较正式。句首 Not until 后，从句保持 I joined，主句倒装为 did I feel，不能把从句也倒装。写作只有需要强调时才使用。

### 2. when 从句与被压缩的选择空间

**原创例句：** When staying becomes dangerous, leaving may be the only realistic option.

中文：用条件性情境解释行动，适合口语 Part 3 和社会议题写作。may 保留边界，不能直接把所有人口迁移都说成被迫。option 常搭配 realistic、viable、limited；避免 only one choices 这类数的一致错误。

### 3. what 从句：把评价对象说清楚

**原创例句：** What helped me most was the opportunity to contribute to a shared task.

中文：what 引导整体作主语，后面 was 与其作为一件事情对应。to contribute 修饰 opportunity；contribute to 后接名词或动名词。口语可自然讲个人经历，写作需说明具体群体和结果。

### 4. although 与让步限定

**原创例句：** Although financial assistance can meet urgent needs, social participation may also be necessary for long-term inclusion.

中文：承认援助作用，再补充另一个维度。although 后不再用 but；also 避免将两种支持写成互相排斥。适合 Academic Writing Task 2，口语可将 financial assistance 换成 money to cover basic costs。

### 5. feel sorry for 与 believe in：情绪和信任的区别

**原创例句：** I appreciated her concern, but I also needed someone to believe in my ability to learn.

中文：feel sorry for someone 表示同情；believe in someone 或 someone's ability 表示信任其潜力。believe someone 通常指相信某人说的话。二者可共存，不能把表达信任理解成无需提供实际支持。适合口语；学术写作可用 recognise people's capabilities。

### 6. belong to 与 a sense of belonging

**原创例句：** Joining the choir gave newcomers a sense of belonging before they felt confident speaking in public.

中文：belong to a group 表示属于群体；feel that you belong 表示感觉被接纳，通常不用 feel belonging。a sense of belonging 适合教育和社区类写作；belongings 是个人物品，不能混用。口语、写作均可。

### 7. integrate into 与 assimilate：融入不等于放弃身份

**原创例句：** Language classes can help residents integrate into local life while maintaining their cultural traditions.

中文：常用 integrate into a community；integration into society。assimilate 常涉及对主流文化的趋同，在文化议题中语义更强。不要默认社会融入必须删除原有文化。正式写作适用；口语可说 take part in local life。

### 8. refugee、migrant、displaced person：范围不同

**原创例句：** The report distinguishes refugees from people displaced within their own country rather than placing everyone in a single category.

中文：refugee 与 asylum seeker 的身份也有区别；displaced person 是更宽泛的描述，migrant 的使用范围取决于语境。这些是阅读时应分清的类别，不把不同群体的数据直接相加或替换。学术写作应先定义范围，本文不提供法律资格判定。

### 9. provide someone with 与 provide something for

**原创例句：** The centre provides new arrivals with language support and creates opportunities for local residents to volunteer.

中文：provide someone with something，或 provide something for someone；不写 provide someone something。support 泛指支持时不可数。适合写作，口语也可说 gives people practical help；不要为了正式而把帮助者写成唯一行动主体。

### 10. resilience、vulnerability、capability：避免单一标签

**原创例句：** Recognising a family's resilience should not prevent us from addressing the barriers it still faces.

中文：resilience 是受挫后应对和恢复的能力；vulnerability 强调易受伤害处境；capability 强调能够做什么。prevent someone from doing 是固定结构。适合正式写作，口语可用 ability to recover；有韧性不意味着不需要支持。

### 11. account、evidence、proof：按证据强度选词

**原创例句：** This personal account illustrates how exclusion can affect confidence, but it does not establish how common the experience is.

中文：account 在此是叙述，illustrate 是说明一种可能；不能只靠个案 prove that everyone…。evidence 通常不可数，写 a piece of evidence，不写 an evidence。适合 Academic Writing 与 Part 3，有助于控制概括范围。

### 12. restore confidence 与 regain confidence：施动者不同

**原创例句：** A meaningful role helped restore his confidence, and he gradually regained confidence in his own judgement.

中文：restore confidence 常强调某事使信心恢复；regain confidence 强调人重新获得信心。搭配 confidence in someone/something；confident about doing something。口语、写作均可。不要用 recover confident，confident 是形容词。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

结构可概括为：课堂开场 → 当年的全球规模 → 家族与个人经历 → 接纳的具体体验 → 足球建立关系 → 家庭故事呈现代价与能力 → 反歧视倡议 → 回到学生和尊严。足球先出现在童年营地，再出现在社区球队，连接“自己学会尊重别人”和“后来实践这种尊重”。课堂则构成首尾呼应。

证据层次需要分清：祖母、餐馆和球队属于个人经历；叙利亚母亲及学生家庭属于讲者转述的见证；人数和安置比例属于当时统计性陈述，本文未独立核查其口径；社会应怎样对待难民属于价值立场。演讲没有给出对照研究，不能声称它已证明某种社区项目必然改善所有人的长期生活。

衔接可用三句原创框架练习：A personal experience reveals one part of the problem. / The next example shows how practical opportunities can matter. / These stories support a moral concern, although wider outcomes require further evidence. 它们分别执行引入、推进和限定；写作时须填入具体机制，不能只背连接句。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习1｜60秒复述：关系如何帮助融入

闭卷用自己的话说清：一个困难、一个支持方式、一个可能变化、一个证据限制。用餐馆或球队其中一个例子即可，不要求记数字或人名。

**原创参考答案：**

Mufleh describes how losing a familiar home can weaken a person's confidence. In her own experience, a restaurant job offered more than income: it gave her responsibility and contact with a welcoming household. Her football team later created another route into community life. These stories suggest that people need opportunities to participate as well as practical support. However, personal experiences alone cannot tell us which programme will work best for every community.

中文：先解释问题，再说明工作和球队的可能机制，最后限定证据。按自然速度约45–60秒，不追求逐字一致。

### 练习2｜三句迁移：学校里的新同学

不用难民主题，写三句谈帮助转校生：一句 although 让步；一句 provide…with…；一句 a sense of belonging。说明具体行动，避免“大家都一定成功”。

**原创参考答案：**

Although a welcome pack explains school rules, it may not help a new student make friends. A peer mentoring programme can provide newcomers with regular opportunities to ask questions. Working on a shared project may gradually give them a sense of belonging.

中文：信息支持和关系支持互相补充；provide 的介词结构正确；may 和 gradually 控制结果强度。这套表达可迁移到教育类写作。

### 练习3｜口语 Part 3：社区怎样帮助新居民

用60–90秒回答：What can communities do to help new residents feel included? 至少包含一项行动、作用机制和现实限制。首次作答后再展开参考答案。

**原创参考答案：**

I think communities can start with activities in which people have a useful role. For example, a local garden could invite new residents to share planting skills rather than simply attend a welcome event. Working together gives people repeated contact and something concrete to discuss. Language support is also important, because an invitation is of limited use if someone cannot understand it. Still, participation should be voluntary, and organisers need to consider working hours and childcare. Different residents will need different routes into community life.

中文：先给观点，再以花园解释“有角色—反复接触—交流”的机制，补上语言、时间和照护限制。答案是原创迁移，不是演讲中的项目介绍。
