## 二、分段双语内容导读

### 00:04–00:52｜把习以为常的不便重新变成问题

**原创英文概述：** Moreno challenges the idea that residents must organise their lives around inconvenient urban layouts. He makes time lost in travel a question of design and asks planners to reconsider whose needs the built environment serves.

**中文解释：** 开场从城市居民的日常时间损失切入，连续设问把“忍受不便”改写成“规划是否可以改变”。这是问题重构与价值立场，不是已给出数据的通勤研究。听时抓住谁在适应谁这一关系，而不只记住堵车。

### 00:52–01:52｜定义可在附近完成日常生活的城市

**原创英文概述：** His alternative places ordinary activities within a short journey on foot or by bicycle. The proposal connects where people live with access to employment, essential services and opportunities to enjoy their surroundings.

**中文解释：** 此段定义方案：以日常活动的可达性衡量城市，范围包括工作、住房、饮食、健康、教育、文化和休闲。重点是服务组合与出行时间，不是地图上画一个固定半径；同样距离对步行者与骑车者的时间可能不同。

### 01:52–02:33｜用另一种街道图景说明改变的价值

**原创英文概述：** A street imagined as a place for greenery and everyday encounters offers a concrete alternative to traffic and pollution. The contrast links neighbourhood design with fairness, personal welfare and environmental responsibility.

**中文解释：** 讲者邀请听众想象能步行购物、上学和相遇的街道，使抽象规划变成生活画面。例子的作用是展示可能性；它不是某条街改造前后的测量结果。随后把改革理由从方便扩大到公正、福祉与气候。

### 02:33–03:42｜先盘点空间和服务，再讨论改造

**原创英文概述：** He proposes an audit of existing places: what they contain, who uses them and which needs remain unmet nearby. Questions about local facilities and the separation of home from work turn the broad vision into practical planning tasks.

**中文解释：** 逻辑从愿景落到调查：空间给谁用、怎么用，服务是否只集中在中心区，社区是否有绿地和应对热浪的设施，以及居住和工作为何相距很远。这里是一组诊断问题，不是要求先大规模拆建；雅思写作可借鉴“先查缺口再提投入”。

### 03:42–04:21｜用四条原则组织方案

**原创英文概述：** Environmental care, shorter journeys, social connection and resident involvement form his organising framework. Together, they make neighbourhood improvement a social project as well as a matter of physical layout.

**中文解释：** 四原则分别对应生态、接近性、团结互助与参与。列举帮助听众分类记忆，也提醒规划不只是设施距离：居民之间的联系和参与过程同样进入评价。复述时给每条原则配一个自己的解释，避免只背四个名词。

### 04:21–05:03｜回应误解，并把边缘地区纳入目标

**原创英文概述：** Moreno clarifies that local convenience can coexist with the energy and creativity of a large city. He then extends the promise of nearby services to people outside the centre, making inclusion a central condition of the proposal.

**中文解释：** 先回应听众可能把方案理解成乡村化的疑问，保留城市活力，再强调中心与边缘居民都应受益。这是预先回应反对意见和明确价值条件。不能因为一个街区很方便，就断定全城服务公平。

### 05:03–05:30｜把巴黎作为政策入口

**原创英文概述：** Paris provides a named example of how the idea might enter public policy. In his account of the mayor's proposals, strengthening district-level services is a way to distribute daily opportunities more widely.

**中文解释：** 讲者把巴黎描述为率先采用理念的城市，并介绍当时市长 Anne Hidalgo 的邻近服务方案。这里按演讲归属报告其说法，不把“率先”当成本篇独立核验的历史排名。05:28 官方稿标注城市声音，05:30 转回措施；没有据此声称已经听过音效。

### 05:30–06:21｜让现有设施承担更多用途

**原创英文概述：** Cycling infrastructure, neighbourhood businesses and greener spaces accompany a proposal to reuse existing buildings at different times. Opening facilities to additional activities illustrates how a city could improve access without relying entirely on new construction.

**中文解释：** 学校夜间作为社区活动场所、体育设施加入制作空间等例子，把空间复用讲得具体。举例支持“同一空间可以承载不同功能”的规划主张，未提供节约成本或改善健康的量化结果。安全管理、人员和维护成本是后续评估问题，不是本段已解决的细节。

### 06:21–06:57｜把措施压缩成可记忆的设计标准

**原创英文概述：** He gathers the proposal into three practical tests: whether planning fits human routines, whether places support varied uses and whether residents can meet ordinary needs with fewer trips beyond their neighbourhood.

**中文解释：** 这一段重新收束前面的细节，形成以人安排节奏、空间多用、减少经常性远行三项标准。三项特征与前面的四原则层次不同：前者描述设计效果，后者组织价值与过程，复述时不要机械混成七条同类规定。

### 06:57–07:40｜以时间悖论回扣开场

**原创英文概述：** The ending questions a model of efficiency that promises speed while creating lengthy journeys. Moreno closes by proposing that bringing activities closer may recover time more effectively than continually trying to travel faster.

**中文解释：** 结尾回到开场的时间损失，把“交通更快”与“生活真正省时”区分开来，完成闭环。这里是对规划思路的反思，不是每种交通方式的实测比较。07:40 的致谢结束主要内容。

## 三、深入语言分析

以下语言项目由话题延伸，完整英文句子均为原创；不声称每项表达都出现在逐字稿中。优先练准含义、介词与论断力度，再增加复杂度。

### 1. 目的从句：so that + 主语 + can

**原创例句：** The library could extend its opening hours so that people who work late can use it.

中文：解释措施如何服务具体人群，适合 Task 2 与 Part 3。so that 后有完整主谓；不要写 so that to use。与 because 不同，它说明目的，并不保证目标已经实现。

### 2. 被动结构与责任主体：be designed to

**原创例句：** A public square should be designed to accommodate older residents as well as children.

中文：被动语态适合突出设施的功能；正式写作可用，口语也自然。to 后接动词原形。需要讨论责任时，可改成 Local authorities should design…，避免全文被动导致执行者消失。

### 3. 让步与可行性：although / provided that

**原创例句：** Although shared facilities can save space, they work well only provided that maintenance responsibilities are clear.

中文：前半承认优点，后半提出实施条件，适合政策类写作。provided that 相当于“只要／条件是”，语域较正式；口语可用 if。although 后不再加 but；can 表示可能效用，不等于已经证明有效。

### 4. 反事实设想：if + 过去式，would + 原形

**原创例句：** If my neighbourhood had a reliable evening bus service, I would leave the car at home more often.

中文：用第二条件句描述与当前情况不同的设想，适合 Part 2、Part 3。had 在这里不是过去时间的叙事；不要在 if 从句里机械加入 would。它表达个人判断，不证明所有人会同样改变。

### 5. have access to / access

**原创例句：** Residents need access to affordable childcare, and they should be able to access information about available places easily.

中文：名词 access 常用 access to，通常不可数；动词 access 直接接宾语，不写 access to information 作动词结构。写作讨论服务机会很适用；“附近有”不必然等于价格、名额和时间上都能使用。

### 6. within walking distance of / in the vicinity of

**原创例句：** I live within walking distance of a clinic, but there is no pharmacy in the vicinity of my office.

中文：前者表示可步行到达，口语写作均可；后者表示在附近，较正式，日常说 near 更简洁。固定搭配中的介词为 of；within walking distance 不是固定十五分钟，需按上下文说明。

### 7. commute / travel / journey

**原创例句：** My daily commute takes forty minutes, although the journey is usually shorter during school holidays.

中文：commute 指规律的上下班或上学往返，可作动词或可数名词；journey 指一次行程；travel 作一般“旅行／出行”名词通常不可数。口语说 commute to work 或 commute from A to B，不写 commute my office；不要把所有购物行程都叫 commute。

### 8. make use of / underused

**原创例句：** The council could make better use of an underused hall by offering evening classes there.

中文：make use of 后接资源；by doing 解释利用方式。underused 指使用不足，未必完全空置；unused 更接近尚未使用。适合正式提案；underused 本身需要判断依据，不能看见一次空房间就认定长期浪费。

### 9. involve someone in / participation in

**原创例句：** Planners should involve tenants in decisions about shared gardens rather than consult only property owners.

中文：involve someone in something 表示让人参与，participation in 是名词结构；适合城市与教育写作。不要写 involve residents to decisions。participate in 不及物，不写 participate the project。例句中的租客建议为原创扩展。

### 10. proximity / accessibility / availability

**原创例句：** Proximity to a hospital does not guarantee accessibility if the entrance has steps, while the availability of appointments presents a separate problem.

中文：proximity 重在空间接近，accessibility 重在实际能否到达或使用，availability 重在资源是否可获得。三词适合 Academic 写作，不能为避免重复随意互换。常用 proximity to、accessibility of、availability of；例子以台阶和预约说明三者差别。

### 11. reduce / alleviate / eliminate

**原创例句：** More frequent buses may alleviate congestion, but they are unlikely to eliminate all delays.

中文：reduce 是一般减少，alleviate 是缓解负担或问题，eliminate 是消除，力度最强。适合 Task 2；口语可用 ease traffic problems。不要把缓解拥堵写成保证零延误；alleviate 后直接接宾语，不加 from。

### 12. at the expense of / strike a balance between

**原创例句：** Cities need to strike a balance between commercial activity and quiet residential space, rather than expand nightlife at the expense of residents' sleep.

中文：前者表示以牺牲另一事物为代价；后者表示兼顾两项需求，适合写作与 Part 3。between A and B 保持名词结构平行；不要写 balance between A with B。提出平衡后还需说明措施，不能靠这个词组代替论证。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

**论证路线：** 日常时间损失 → 重新定义规划目标 → 街道对照图景 → 资源与服务盘点 → 四原则 → 回应乡村化误解及公平要求 → 巴黎与空间复用例子 → 三特征 → 回扣时间悖论。

**例子与证据层级：** 街道图景是设想，帮助听众看见可能性；巴黎是讲者介绍的政策案例，使方案显得可操作；学校与体育设施的复用是机制说明。这些内容没有构成政策成效的因果研究。演讲也没有以系统个人经历作为主要证据。复述时可分别使用 he proposes、he illustrates、he describes，避免把所有内容都说成 research proves。

**衔接训练：** 先用设问建立问题，再定义解决目标；用列举组织原则，用澄清回应误解，最后总结特征。写作中可迁移为“问题—措施—机制—限制”的一段：设施缺口是什么、如何补充、为什么可能有帮助、什么条件仍须满足。不要为了模仿演讲，在学术作文里连续堆积反问句。

**跟读练习建议，未核验声音特征：** 回放 03:42–04:21 的原则列表，自选约十五秒听两遍；第一遍标出自己实际听到的意群边界，第二遍录音模仿，再比较遗漏词与语速。这里不预先断言讲者在哪里重读、升调或停顿。

**原创替换练习：** A useful neighbourhood / offers essential services nearby / and gives residents a voice in local decisions.

斜线仅为建议分组，不是官方停顿标注。先分组读清楚，再自然连贯地读；把 essential services 替换成自己的具体需求。目标是稳定表达思想，不追求复制讲者口音。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习 1｜一分钟复述：从问题走到方案

闭卷用 70–100 个英文词解释讲者要解决什么、方案怎样运作、一个例子及一个证据限制。避免把政策提案说成已被证明的结果。

**原创参考答案：**

Moreno argues that urban design can make everyday life unnecessarily time-consuming. He proposes placing essential activities closer to where people live, so that shorter trips become practical. Existing buildings could also serve different groups at different times; a school, for example, might host community activities after lessons. His approach combines environmental goals with social connection and resident involvement. However, the talk presents a planning vision and selected examples, rather than a controlled evaluation. We would need further evidence to judge its effects on different neighbourhoods.

中文解释：答案覆盖问题、机制、例子、原则与证据边界；might 和 would need 保持论断力度。复述不必列完所有服务，优先说明“为何就近与复用可能节约时间”。

### 练习 2｜造句迁移：把空泛建议变成措施

把“城市应该更方便”改写成两句具体英文：第一句使用 so that 与 access to，第二句用 involve … in 并指出一个需参与的人群。

**原创参考答案：**

The council could open a small health centre near the housing estate so that older residents have access to routine care without a long bus journey. It should involve people with limited mobility in decisions about entrances and seating.

中文解释：写清设施、对象与目的，再补充参与程序。routine care 限定基础服务，不暗示小型中心能提供全部专科治疗；in 与 to 各司其职。检查自己是否只换了形容词，却仍未说明行动。

### 练习 3｜IELTS Part 3：是否应让学校供社区夜间使用？

用约一分钟回答：Should schools be used for community activities in the evening? 给出立场、原因、例子和一项执行条件；不必引用演讲。

**原创参考答案：**

I think this can be a sensible use of public space, especially where residents have few affordable places to meet. A school hall could host language classes or a local reading group after pupils leave. This might broaden access to learning without requiring a separate building. However, sharing space is not cost-free. Someone must organise bookings, cleaning and security, and the school still needs suitable time to prepare for the next day. I would support a limited trial with clear responsibilities before extending the arrangement.

中文解释：先支持，再用具体活动解释好处；转折补充运营成本，结尾给出试行方案。limited trial 是原创评估建议，不是讲者报告的项目；避免把多功能空间等同于免费空间。
