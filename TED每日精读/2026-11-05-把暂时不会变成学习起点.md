## 三、深入语言分析

以下例句全部原创。优先掌握“表达关系准确、搭配自然、证据强度适当”，再追求词汇变化。

### 1. have not … yet：未完成不等于不可能

**原创例句：** I have not learned to organise a clear argument yet, so I am practising one paragraph at a time.

中文：现在完成时表示截至目前的状态，yet 常在否定句末。适合口语 Part 1/2 的学习经历；写作可用 has not yet achieved。不要写 I haven't can；若强调能力，用 I cannot do it yet。还要给出具体行动，不能把 yet 当成结果保证。

### 2. interpret A as B：说明人怎样理解事件

**原创例句：** Some students interpret a low mark as a judgement of their potential rather than feedback on a particular task.

中文：interpret something as something；as 不能随意换成 to。a judgement of 在此指对潜力的评价。适合 Task 2 与口语 Part 3，口语也可说 see a low mark as…。rather than 后两项都是名词短语，保持平行。

### 3. what matters is …：聚焦评价标准

**原创例句：** What matters is whether learners can use feedback to improve their next attempt.

中文：what 从句作主语，whether 从句作表语，提出真正要考察的标准。适合口语和议论文。不要写 What matters are whether…；更不要只说努力重要而不说明如何检验改进。

### 4. those who …：用关系从句明确比较对象

**原创例句：** Those who receive specific guidance may revise more effectively than those who receive only a score.

中文：两边用 those who 保持比较对象一致；may 保留条件。适合学术写作，口语可说 students who…。不要把“学生”与“成绩”直接比较，也不要凭假设写出确定因果。

### 5. reward someone for doing：奖励什么行为

**原创例句：** A teacher could reward students for explaining how they corrected an error.

中文：reward someone for something/doing something；reward 是奖励，award 常指授予奖项。适合教育类 Task 2；口语可说 give students credit for…。不能写 reward students to explain 来表示奖励原因；to explain 容易被理解为目的。

### 6. engage with 与 engage in：对象和活动

**原创例句：** Learners engage with written feedback when they use it to revise; they engage in discussion when they compare possible solutions.

中文：engage with 强调认真处理某对象，engage in 强调参与活动。两者并非在所有语境中互斥，这里展示最便于迁移的区别。写作适用；口语用 work through the feedback 或 join the discussion 更自然。engagement 是名词，不能写 students are engagement。

### 7. effort、strategy、progress：投入与结果分开

**原创例句：** More effort produced little progress until I changed my revision strategy.

中文：put effort into doing something，make progress in something，adopt/change a strategy。progress 通常不可数，不写 many progresses。口语和写作都能用；句子也提醒投入时长、采用方法和实际进步不是同一指标。

### 8. persevere with 与 persist in：坚持的对象要具体

**原创例句：** She persevered with a demanding reading task but changed her method when repeated guessing failed.

中文：persevere with a task、persevere in doing something；persist in doing 也可表示坚持，有时含固执意味。persistence/perseverance 是名词。口语可说 keep working on…。不要把坚持误解为永远重复同一失败策略；demanding 在此指要求高，不是提出要求的动作。

### 9. attribute A to B：区分归因与证明

**原创例句：** We should not attribute every improvement in attainment to a change in mindset without considering other classroom changes.

中文：attribute an outcome to a factor；不能把原因和结果倒放。attainment 指达到的学习水平或成就，achievement 范围更宽。适合学术写作；口语可说 assume that one change caused all the improvement。without considering 为论证增加明确边界。

### 10. suggest、demonstrate、prove：控制断言强度

**原创例句：** The classroom example suggests a possible benefit, but it does not prove that the same approach works equally well everywhere.

中文：suggest 表示提示或支持一种可能；demonstrate 通常需要更直接展示；prove 是更强的确证。适合 Task 2 和 Part 3。evidence 不可数；不要为了显得自信，把故事写成 universal proof。suggest 后可接 that 从句，不写 suggest someone to do。

### 11. percentile 与 percentage：排名位置与比例

**原创例句：** A score at the eightieth percentile describes relative standing; it does not mean that the learner answered eighty per cent of the questions correctly.

中文：percentile 描述参照群体中的位置，percentage 表示比例；不能混用。适合 Academic Task 1 或阅读统计信息。还应检查比较群体和测试是否一致；名次上升并不能单独告诉我们实际提升了多少能力。

### 12. provide … with 与 access to：把环境支持写具体

**原创例句：** Schools can provide learners with useful feedback, while equal access to qualified teachers remains essential.

中文：provide someone with something；access to 后接名词，access 作动词时直接接宾语，如 access learning materials。适合教育与公平类写作；口语可说 make sure everyone can get help。qualified teachers 比泛泛写 good resources 更具体，避免把成绩差全归咎于个人态度。

## 四、论证、衔接与跟读建议

论证路线为：评分例子提出问题 → 儿童反应建立概念对比 → 多项研究转述解释行为 → 提问扩大到评价文化 → 反馈和游戏提出做法 → 干预与学校案例说明可能效果 → 来信恢复个人尺度 → 环境与机会的价值倡议。开头的评价方式和结尾的发展环境形成呼应。

证据层次：开场学校做法是引入性例子；讲者早期任务是研究经历；作弊意向、脑电、游戏与成绩变化是研究转述；学校排名是案例报告；雇主反馈和少年来信是见证；所有儿童应有发展机会是价值判断。本文没有独立复核论文，不能据此给出普遍效应大小，或声称个人心态足以抵消资源差异。面向IELTS，关键是解释机制并限制范围。

衔接练习用三句原创框架：One way to understand the problem is to examine the feedback learners receive. / This example shows how the principle could influence classroom practice. / However, wider claims require evidence from different settings. 分别执行缩小焦点、由原则转向做法、限定外推；实际答题时补充具体内容，避免只有连接词。

**跟读练习建议（未核验讲者声音特征）：** 从03:57附近选约25秒，先听一遍，再按官方稿检查功能词及并列项。自己划分“建议—具体行为—预期作用”的意群，跟读并录音一次；最后不用原句解释怎样给一篇作文提供反馈。这是学习者的意群和表达练习，不是对讲者重音、停顿或语调的实测描述。

## 五、练习与参考答案

### 练习1｜60秒复述：观点、例子、限制

闭卷说明演讲核心观点，给一个做法，再用一句话限定证据。不要背逐字稿。

**原创参考答案：** Dweck argues that learners may respond more constructively to difficulty when they see ability as something that can develop. One practical approach is to give feedback on strategies and improvement instead of attaching a fixed label to a student. For example, a teacher could ask a learner to explain how a revised method helped solve a problem. However, the classroom stories in the talk do not establish that mindset alone can overcome differences in teaching quality or resources.

中文解释：先用 may 控制断言，再给具体反馈做法，最后点出外推边界；复述包含主张、机制与限制，比罗列故事更清楚。

### 练习2｜造句：从空泛鼓励改为可执行反馈

为“阅读做了很多仍错得多”写两句英文。第一句用 effort 与 progress 区分投入和结果；第二句用 reward … for 或 engage with 提出行动。

**原创参考答案：** I put considerable effort into reading practice, but I made limited progress because I rarely analysed my mistakes. This week, I will engage with each correction by identifying the clue in the passage and explaining why the other options are wrong.

中文解释：put effort into、make progress 搭配准确；第二句把“认真对待反馈”落实为寻找证据和排除选项。不是简单要求增加题量，也不预设错题只有一个原因。

### 练习3｜口语 Part 3：学校应更重视努力还是成绩？

准备20秒，回答约60–75秒。承认两者作用，给一个例子，并说明资源支持的重要性。

**原创参考答案：** I think schools need to consider both, because they tell us different things. Results show what a student can currently do, while effort can reveal how seriously the student is trying. However, praising effort without looking at the method may encourage unproductive repetition. If a pupil keeps making the same mistakes in maths, the teacher could recognise the persistence and then demonstrate a different strategy. I would also keep some common assessments, since students need clear information about their current understanding. At the same time, fair expectations require access to good teaching and useful feedback. A positive attitude can help, but it cannot replace those conditions.

中文解释：结构为立场—区分指标—限制—例子—补充条件；数学例子为原创。口语用 I think、could 保持自然，避免堆砌学术名词。答案提供思路，实际表达用自己的例子。
